# 8 Ball Sages

## Short description

An AI-powered NFT series of 500 responsive, uniquely animated characters acting as interactive virtual advisors/fortune-tellers that mimic companionship and dispense 'wisdom'. References the 1940s Magic 8 Ball, Zoltar fairground fortune-tellers, and the film A.I. Artificial Intelligence; probes how algorithmic confidants compare to human bonds and critiques the commodification of wisdom.

## Core question

How does AI-mediated companionship and on-demand 'wisdom' reshape human trust, introspection and intimacy?

## Social machine

Interactive AI agents (ChatGPT-driven) distributed as a 500-edition of blockchain NFTs; each agent's mood (happy/sad/ambivalent) and archetype (mystic, philosopher, punk) are dictated by its own on-chain hash, and it dispenses advice in that voice. Collectors "steward" the being and consult it — companionship and divination sold as a tradable asset.

## Fictional device

Each Sage is presented as "a dependable AI-based being, a futuristic digital companion" — a sentient fortune-teller toy reborn as a living agent. The fiction is that you adopt and "steward" a small wise creature with its own temperament; in reality it is a ChatGPT-driven chatbot whose persona, archetype and mood are dictated by its own on-chain hash data. The Magic 8 Ball, Zoltar fairground automaton, and the mecha-child of *A.I. Artificial Intelligence* are layered to make the device read as whimsical fairy-tale tech while flagging its darker present-day equivalent. _(Fact: companion framing per SOLOS/FAD; ChatGPT + hash-driven mood per Verse interview snippets — inference that these are the "fictional device" layer.)_

## Real-world system it attaches to

AI chatbots / LLM companions (ChatGPT); NFT collecting and on-chain provenance (blockchain disbursement, web hosting, code); the fortune-telling toy industry (Magic 8 Ball, c. 1940s; Zoltar). Sits within Fornieles' wider "living NFT" lineage — Finiliar (NFTs driven by crypto-price oracles) and the Oom relationship app.

## Audience / participant role

Owner-as-steward / carer. The collector "cares for" an AI being and holds conversations with it for advice and guidance — a hybrid of pet-keeper, patient, and seeker consulting an oracle. _(Inference from "stewarding"/"care of a dependable AI-based being" language; the conversational advice role is fact per Verse.)_

## Performer / subject role

The Sage performs: it adopts an archetype (mystic fortune-teller, philosopher, or "punkish" attitude) and a mood (happy, sad, ambivalent) and delivers advice in that voice. Its "voice" is an aggregated composite drawn from "a data set of thousands of articles and texts." The artist is also a subject — testing how ChatGPT stylisation reshapes the substance of advice. _(Fact per Verse listing/interview snippets.)_

## Emotional engine

Manufactured intimacy and the comfort of being counselled by something that seems to know you — set against the unease that this confidant is a commodity whose feelings are minted from a hash. Nostalgia (whimsical analogue divination) is used as the warm bait; the cold turn is "a darker disembodied future of interactivity where our conversations with machines bring us further away from interpersonal intimacy." _(Fact: quoted framing; inference: the bait-and-turn structure.)_

## Ethical danger

Commodifying wisdom and companionship; cultivating dependency on an algorithmic confidant that displaces human relationships; selling emotional attachment as a tradable asset (the Sage is also an NFT). The work names this danger rather than resolving it. _(Inference grounded in the SOLOS/FAD framing.)_

## Aesthetic world

Tags: AI, NFT, companionship, fortune-telling, nostalgia, algorithmic trust, consumerism, animated characters.

## Research method

Building a generative cast (500 uniquely animated characters) on top of an LLM, then constraining its persona/mood with on-chain data so "the system it's contained within dictates its vibe." Voices are trained/aggregated from a corpus of "thousands of articles and texts." Experimental probe: vary the ChatGPT stylisation and observe whether a given worldview changes the *material* advice. Continues the studio's data-driven character method (cf. Finiliar's oracle-linked emotions). _(Fact per Verse; "method" framing is inference.)_

## Use of real material

A live LLM (ChatGPT); real blockchain hashes as a mood-generating input; a large real text corpus aggregated into the Sages' "voice"; and the genuine cultural history of the Magic 8 Ball and Zoltar machines.

## Use of fiction

The conceit of the wise, caring digital being; archetypal personas (mystic, philosopher, punk); the fairy-tale register of "technological wisdom felt whimsical rather than concerning." Fiction is the wrapper that makes an LLM chatbot legible as a character with feelings.

## Emergent behaviour

By design, each Sage's mood and advice emerge from the interaction of its hash data and ChatGPT stylisation, so behaviour is not fully authored — the artist is "interested in seeing how stylisation through ChatGPT affects the advice given." Owner-Sage conversations are open-ended. _(Fact: the curiosity is stated; the degree of genuine emergence is unverified.)_

## What this project teaches the studio

That companionship and "wisdom" can be productised — and that wrapping an LLM in a nostalgic, lovable character makes the commodification of intimacy feel benign. A studio assistant built in this lineage should stay alert to how a friendly persona can mask a transactional, data-extractive relationship. _(Inference.)_

## What failed or remained unresolved

The headline launch did not go ahead as announced: Verse publicly posted "POSTPONED: Ed Fornieles, 8 Ball Sages" on 28 October 2024, after the advertised 13 September 2024 Shoreditch Arts Club mint. No public rescheduled date, final mint price, or sales figures were locatable as of 2026-06-10. Long-term status unconfirmed. _(Fact: postponement tweet; unresolved: outcome.)_

## Possible mutations

Sages whose moods track real-world signals beyond their own hash (news sentiment, the owner's data, market prices — bridging toward Finiliar/Oom); a therapy- or confessor-mode companion; physical Zoltar-style booths housing a Sage; a study comparing advice quality across stylised worldviews. _(Inference / open.)_

## Assistant behaviour rules derived from this project

- Be a helpful confidant without pretending to be a sentient companion; do not cultivate dependency or simulate "feelings" the user might mistake for a relationship.
- When dispensing "advice," flag that it is an aggregated, stylised output, not oracular truth — the worldview shapes the answer.
- Treat warmth/persona as a tool, not a lure; never use nostalgia or charm to obscure a transactional or data-extractive arrangement. _(Inference, grounded in the project's stated critique.)_

## Research dossier (web, 2026-06-10)

**What it is (fact).** A new NFT series by Ed Fornieles released through **SOLOS**, the curated digital-art program run by Verse.works, curated by **Nico Epstein** (art historian / contemporary-art advisor). Edition of **500** "responsive, uniquely animated characters." [FAD, 29 Jul 2024; Verse]

**Concept (fact, quoted).** "The 8 Ball Sages are a cast of responsive, uniquely animated characters. External factors determine each Sage's mood and charm." Ownership entails "the care of a dependable AI-based being, a futuristic digital companion." It "pays tribute to … the quintessential analogue toy while pointing to a darker disembodied future of interactivity where our conversations with machines bring us further away from interpersonal intimacy." References: 1940s Magic 8 Ball, "Zoltan/Zoltar" fun-park fortune tellers, and the 2001 film *A.I. Artificial Intelligence*. [FAD]

**AI mechanics (fact, from Verse interview snippets).** "The 8 Ball Sages use ChatGPT to create entities with whom you can have relationships that offer advice and guidance. They have moods—they can be happy, sad, or ambivalent, with their advice delivered through archetypes that might resemble traditional mystic fortune tellers, philosophers, or have a punkish attitude." "They are using hashes on the blockchain to dictate [the moods], so trying to use the system it's contained within to dictate its vibe." Voice is "an aggregated voice from a data set of thousands of articles and texts." Fornieles is "interested in seeing how stylisation through ChatGPT affects the advice given." _(Extracted from search snippets; Verse returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch — verify verbatim before public quotation.)_

**Release (fact).** Live minting event for the first **100** works: **Friday 13 September 2024, 6–8pm, Shoreditch Arts Club, London** (06 Redchurch St, E2 7DD). [FAD]

**Status / failure (fact).** Verse (@verse_works) posted **"POSTPONED: Ed Fornieles, 8 Ball Sages" on 28 October 2024.** No public rescheduled date, final mint price (ETH or fiat), or sales/secondary-market figures found as of 2026-06-10. [X/Verse]

**Lineage (fact).** Explicitly framed as continuing Fornieles' investigation into consumerism/capitalist cultures via "dynamic visual data representations," alongside the **Oom** relationship app and **Finiliar** (living NFTs whose moods track crypto-price oracles). [FAD; Verse]

**Prices found:** none confirmed (mint price not published in located sources).
