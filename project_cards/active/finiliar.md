# Finiliar

## Short description

'Living' digital creatures whose moods change in real time according to the price of a linked cryptocurrency. The 2022 NFT collection is 10,000 PFPs across ten 'families', each tethered via data oracles to a specific coin (ETH, BTC, BNB, SOL, AVAX, DOGE, MATIC, LINK, UNI, XTZ): when the coin rises the creature looks happy/energetic, when it falls it looks sad or sick. The concept began in 2016 as a single GIF tied to an RSS currency feed.

## Core question

What is our emotional relationship to the abstract financial/data systems that govern our lives — can data be made to feel cute and alive?

## Social machine

Dynamic NFTs driven by blockchain price oracles; moods update hourly/daily/weekly, with community lore ('Fini World'), a companion app (Oom), and Fini Sketches (2024).

## Fictional device

The Finiliar itself: a cute, anthropomorphic creature — a "familiar" in the witch's-companion / Pullman-dæmon sense — that serves as the living embodiment ("body") of an otherwise bodiless, abstract data stream. Fornieles: "The Finiliar is an attempt to try to give a body to infrastructure or to things that normally don't have an existence in a reality." (inference + source) A wrapping mythology — "Fini World" with ten "families" / ~150 "clans" classified Pokédex-style — fictionalises the crypto-market roster into a bestiary.

## Real-world system it attaches to

Cryptocurrency markets (ten coins: ETH, BTC, BNB, SOL, AVAX, DOGE, MATIC, LINK, UNI, XTZ) via blockchain price oracles; NFT/PFP collecting culture; and — by design intent — extensible to any data feed (stocks, carbon emissions, rainforest, personal health/fitness data). (fact)

## Audience / participant role

Owner / carer / collector. Each holder "adopts" a Fini and watches its mood; Fornieles frames the experience as fostering "empathy and powerlessness" — the individual cannot move the market alone, only collective action can change the data, so the role is simultaneously caring and structurally powerless. NFT distribution lets "a piece of work … have an intimacy with thousands of people, and not just a few." (source)

## Performer / subject role

The creature performs the market: it cavorts, smiles, sobs, sickens or vomits as price moves. The "subject" being portrayed is really capital itself — "the Finiliar is more than a character; it is a manifestation of the market itself" (Mickleburgh, THE SEEN). Holders, secondarily, become performers of attachment within the community. (source)

## Emotional engine

Cuteness as a conduit for empathy toward the impersonal. The kawaii/Tamagotchi face hijacks care instincts so that an abstract number (a coin's price) produces a felt emotional response — joy when it pumps, anxiety/grief when it dumps. Mickleburgh: "If a Finiliar weeps in the corner when Ethereum plummets, is there a human being of flesh and bone somewhere on this Earth doing the same thing?" (source)

## Ethical danger

The same cuteness that builds empathy can launder/aestheticise speculation, gamifying financial risk and binding owners' emotional wellbeing to volatile crypto prices. Fornieles himself flags the manipulability: group emotion "can be manipulated to effect both positive and negative ends." Owning a mood-linked speculative asset risks making real anxiety from real losses feel like pet-care. (inference, grounded in source quotes)

## Aesthetic world

Tags: crypto, NFT, data visualization, cuteness, oracles, digital pets, finance, affect, Tamagotchi.

## Research method

Long-horizon iterative development from a single conceptual artwork (2016 GIF on an RSS currency feed) toward a large-scale participatory system. Production scaled into a 14-person team (≈10 animators), international freelancers, a months-long studio collaboration in Chile, with Lamel as lead 3D artist (consistent since 2016). Engineering wires CoinGecko-style price data through on-chain oracles to drive metadata-linked mood states. (source)

## Use of real material

Live cryptocurrency price data, pulled via oracles, is the literal animating input — the artwork's state is determined by actual market movements at hourly / twice-daily / daily / weekly / (rarest) monthly intervals depending on the token's rarity tier. (fact)

## Use of fiction

A whole worldbuilding layer: "Fini World," ten families, ~150 clans, 11,000+ traits, 1,000+ handmade textures, an "invisible" rarity system discoverable only by eye, and narrative lore (e.g. Dogecoin as a Victorian dog society; a character "Psycho," a serial-killer dog with victims). Humour and narrative make the financial substrate legible and shareable. (source)

## Emergent behaviour

A collector community formed around watching, naming, trading and narrating the creatures — "Suddenly there are 2000 collectors who think about them … part of an ongoing conversation." The companion app **Oom** (TestFlight beta, CoinGecko-equivalent tracking; planned health/step/fitness integration and naming) extends the pet into a self-reflection tool. **Fini Sketches** (2024, 25 sketches/storyboards) documents character development as its own collectible layer. (source)

## What this project teaches the studio

That cuteness is an interface — "bodies and faces are the original interfaces" — and can be weaponised to make abstract, impersonal systems emotionally legible. That a fine-art concept can migrate into Web3 distribution to reach thousands intimately rather than a few. That the financial feed is "low hanging fruit": the real ambition is binding *any* data (climate, health, institutions) to an affective body. (source)

## What failed or remained unresolved

Open / unverified from public sources: long-term holder retention and floor-price decline after the 2022 bull-market peak (floor ~0.22 ETH at one early-2022 snapshot vs 0.06/0.1 ETH mint); how much of the promised roadmap (Oom social/multiplayer, naming, non-crypto data feeds, "training" Finis to new assets) shipped at scale. The ethical tension between empathy-building and speculation-aestheticising remains unresolved by design. (inference — confirm with Ed)

## Possible mutations

Bind a Finiliar to non-financial feeds: carbon emissions, deforestation, a city's air quality, a person's biometric/step data (Fornieles' stated intent). Collective-care mechanics where a community's pooled action visibly heals a creature. Institutional/civic dashboards rendered as creatures. Physical/toy and AR embodiments. (source + inference)

## Assistant behaviour rules derived from this project

When representing data or systems for the studio, prefer an embodied, affective interface (a face, a creature, a mood) over a chart when the goal is empathy or legibility. Be honest that cuteness is persuasive and can mask risk — flag the speculation/manipulation danger rather than hide it. Keep fact (mechanics, dates, coins, contract) cleanly separated from interpretation. (inference)

## Research dossier (web, 2026-06-10)

**The ten coins / families (fact):** Avalanche (AVAX), Binance (BNB), Bitcoin (BTC), Chainlink (LINK), Dogecoin (DOGE), Ethereum (ETH), Polygon (MATIC), Solana (SOL), Tezos (XTZ), Uniswap (UNI). [OpenSea, Bankless]

**Collection facts:** 10,000 ERC-721 NFTs on Ethereum; contract `0x5a0121a0a21232ec0d024dab9017314509026480` (token FINI); series released on Verse 17 Aug 2022; ~150 clans, 11,000+ traits, 1,000+ handmade textures, invisible (off-metadata) rarity. [Verse, Web3 Galaxy Brain]

**Sale mechanics (fact):** Jan 2022 pre-sale of 1,000 eggs @ 0.06 ETH (funded the year of 3D production); March 2022 main sale of 8,790 eggs @ 0.1 ETH, 20-per-tx cap; physical+emotional animations rolled out Q2 2022. [Bankless/Metaversal]

**Mood update tiers (fact):** hourly (commonest) → daily → twice daily → weekly → monthly (rarest). Up = happy/buoyant; down = sad/sick. [Bankless, OpenSea, Verse]

**Origin (fact):** Began 2016 as a single GIF tethered to an RSS currency feed; shown in galleries from New York to Tokyo 2016–2017 (monochrome blob forms + sculptures) before the 2022 NFT relaunch. [OpenSea, Web3 Galaxy Brain]

**Team / collaborators (fact):** Ed Fornieles (creator/artist); Sam Spike (curator, partnered Jan 2022; artistic director, Fingerprints DAO; co-worked on Tim Berners-Lee WWW source-code NFT, Sotheby's 2021) and Ben Vickers (advisors); Jake Allen & Rafael Jegundo (developers; Allen ex-Head of Product at Cameo); Dollar Monopoly (socials); Lamel (lead 3D artist since 2016); ~14-person team incl. ~10 animators; studio stint in Chile. [Bankless, Web3 Galaxy Brain]

**Key quotes:**
- "They were these cute embodiments of data that existed… What I saw in NFTs was the possibility that a piece of work might have an intimacy with thousands of people, and not just a few." — Ed Fornieles [OpenSea]
- "The Finiliar is an attempt to try to give a body to infrastructure or to things that normally don't have an existence in a reality." — Fornieles [Verse / Computerworld]
- "This could also be attached to carbon emissions. It could be attached to the rainforest. And it is a societal responsibility to change the Faniliar, essentially." — Fornieles [Computerworld]
- "bodies and faces are the original interfaces… Finnie's are evolving, quite literally evolving." [Web3 Galaxy Brain]

**Conceptual lineage (source):** Tamagotchi / kawaii culture; the "familiar" (witch's companion) and Philip Pullman's *His Dark Materials* dæmons — "both part of them, but also somehow separate."

**Tools / extensions (fact):** companion app **Oom** (TestFlight beta; CoinGecko-style tracking; planned step/fitness + naming + social features); **Fini Sketches** (2024, 25 sketches/storyboards).

**Inference vs fact:** Coin roster, dates, contract, sale figures, team = fact (multiple public sources). Emotional-engine / ethical-danger framings = inference grounded in Fornieles' own quotes and the THE SEEN review. Floor-price decline / roadmap delivery = unverified.
