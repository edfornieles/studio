# Do Disturb / 'The Fortress of Solitude' (Palais de Tokyo)

## Short description

Ed Fornieles took part in **DO DISTURB — Festival non-stop** (2nd edition), Palais de Tokyo, Paris, 8–10 April 2016 — a non-stop festival of "more than 50 experimental projects" mixing circus, performance, dance, design, fashion and sound. The festival press materials describe his work as exploring "the formatted subjectivity of a generation bottle-fed with web 2.0," but give **no title** for his contribution. Separately, his Somerset House bio states he "organised the performance _The Fortress of Solitude_" at Palais de Tokyo. The two are plausibly the same occasion, but **no single source confirms the link.** (Fact + flagged inference.)

Not documented for the specific Fornieles piece. At the level of his stated theme: how is selfhood "formatted" by having been "bottle-fed with web 2.0"? ## Research dossier (web, 2026-06-10)

**Confirmed (fact):** Fornieles participated in DO DISTURB, Palais de Tokyo, 2nd edition, 8–10 April 2016. Festival = 50+ non-stop experimental projects; all-night event night of 8 Apr (11pm–6am); €15/€12 entry. Festival/PR describe his work as exploring "the formatted subjectivity of a generation bottle-fed with web 2.0." His Carlos/Ishikawa CV lists the festival under 2016 group exhibitions. Separately, Somerset House bio: he "organised the performance _The Fortress of Solitude_" at Palais de Tokyo.

**The Fortress-of-Solitude question (key finding):** UNCONFIRMED that "The Fortress of Solitude" = his Do Disturb 2016 contribution. Reasons: (a) Palais de Tokyo and Claudine Colin Do Disturb sources name Fornieles but give NO performance title; (b) the Somerset House bio names the title but gives NO year and does NOT mention Do Disturb; (c) Wikipedia and the Carlos/Ishikawa CV do not mention "Fortress of Solitude" at all. So it is the *only* documented Fornieles performance at Palais de Tokyo, which makes the equation plausible, but a direct source tying title → festival → April 2016 was not found. Treat as inference.
