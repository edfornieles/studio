# Jupiter Ascending / Sauna Project

## Short description

A sauna/spa-like installation shown in Stockholm, connected to the exhibition
*Jupiter Ascending*. A blacked-out sauna in which distorted news audio became
abstract bass and meditative noise; heat, darkness and intense sound produced a
hallucinatory, altered state. A meditation on news flows and media cycles.

## Core question

What happens when people get lost or immersed in news cycles — when news becomes
the thing that fuels identity? How do media bubbles and polarised news cycles
feel in the body?

## Social machine

The news cycle / media bubble as an immersive environment. The installation
converts media overload into a physiological experience.

## Fictional device

The reframing of a real sauna/spa as a media-processing chamber — news distorted
into meditative noise and visuals, the body as interface.

## Real-world system it attaches to

Contemporary news media, distorted news, media bubbles and polarised news cycles
(intensifying around this period and later, especially around the first Trump
election); the cultural form of the sauna/spa as care and refuge.

## Audience / participant role

A body that enters the sauna and is immersed — subjected to heat, darkness and
distorted-news sound; the participant processes media physiologically rather than
informationally.

## Performer / subject role

The participant's own body is the subject — the site where media becomes heat,
sound, vibration and hallucination.

## Emotional engine

The pull between media overload and the longing for refuge/care; immersion,
meditative dissolution, and the unease of news-as-identity-fuel.

## Ethical danger

Inducing an altered/hallucinatory state via heat, darkness and intense sound;
the seductive blurring of care/refuge with media saturation.

## Aesthetic world

Blacked-out sauna; heat and darkness; distorted news audio pumped in as abstract
bass and meditative noise, with meditative visuals. Tags: sauna, spa, immersion,
news cycles, distorted news, meditative noise, hallucination, media overload,
data stream, body as interface, care, ritual refuge.

## Research method

Translating news video/audio into distorted sound and visuals; designing an
enclosed environmental experience that fuses heat, darkness and sound.

## Use of real material

Real news (video and audio) taken as source and distorted into bass / meditative
noise and visuals.

## Use of fiction

The conceptual reframing of the spa as a media-processing, hallucination-inducing
interface for the body.

## Emergent behaviour

The combination of heat, darkness and intense distorted sound produces altered,
hallucinatory states — felt differently by each body.

## Residue / documentation / afterlife

Documented publicly by ~62 installation photographs on the Carl Kostyál exhibition
page (DSC2362–DSC2753, 2015; catalogued in
image_manifests/_incoming/jupiter-ascending-sauna.jsonl). Public sources confirm
the spa/sweat-lodge, communal and private meditation spaces, per-space "data
totems" (rolling breaking-news, image feeds, graphics) and the live distorted
news-radio audio fed into the sauna; they do not detail the distorted-audio
files themselves or a discrete object checklist. (Fact: photos + features;

## What this project teaches the studio

- News cycle as immersive environment.
- Media as identity fuel.
- Distortion transforms information into bodily experience.
- The installation becomes a physiological interface.
- The body processes media as heat, sound, vibration and hallucination.
- Immersive environments can make abstract systems felt.

## What failed or remained unresolved

Public sources do not resolve this. The gallery framing keeps the tension open
by design — the spa is "a pocket of resistance in which to reflect, review and
dive back into the bombardment of global news," i.e. refuge that returns you to
the overload rather than ending it; and the news audio is deliberately
"distorted" / "abstracted," implying low literal legibility. Whether the
news-source remained recognisable, and whether the refuge/overload tension was
meant to resolve, still needs confirming with Ed. (Inference from gallery text;
`needs_review`.)

## Possible mutations

- A live-news version where the distortion responds to the day's headlines.
- Other bodily interfaces (cold, pressure, scent) as media-processing chambers.
- A personalised version distorting each participant's own feed.

## Assistant behaviour rules derived from this project

- Look for ways to make an abstract system (media, data) physically felt.
- Treat distortion/translation as an operation: information into bodily experience.
- Hold the care/overload tension open rather than resolving it.
- Foreground the altered-state ethics of immersive environmental work.

## Research dossier (web, 2026-06-10)

**Date / venue correction (Fact).** Public sources date the exhibition to
**2015, not c.2016–2017**: *Jupiter Ascending*, Ed Fornieles, Carl Kostyál,
Stockholm (Isbrytaren venue), **19.02.2015 — 20.03.2015**, open Fri/Sat 12–4pm
or by appointment (contact emmahatt@icloud.com). Wikipedia likewise dates it
2015. (Frontmatter `date` left unchanged pending Ed's confirmation; flagged
under `needs_review`.)

**Gallery framing (Fact — Kostyál page).** "A functioning spa exploring themes
of renewal, contemplation and the effects of living in a stream of data… a
pocket of resistance in which to reflect, review and dive back into the
bombardment of global news, bio explosions, eco meltdowns and emotional
matrixes." Aim: to disrupt the high-speed flow of information.

**Sauna / sweat lodge (Fact).** Spa facilities are based on the Native American
sweat lodge — "a place of spiritual refuge used to repair damages done to both
mind and body." "In the darkness of the lodge, all sense of race, colour and
religion are set aside." Live news-radio channels are distorted and fed into the
sauna as "an abstracted background to personal and communal visions."

**Tea ceremony (Fact).** Ancient Japanese teas grown on the volcanic island of
Honshu, used in Zen tea ceremonies, counterbalance caffeine's accelerationist
effect; L-Theanine calms the nervous system while enhancing mindfulness.

**Data totems (Fact).** Communal and private meditation spaces each have a "data
totem" where breaking news, image feeds and graphics roll over visitors — "a
reality stream of impermanent identities forming narratives."

**Documentation (Fact).** ~62 distinct installation photographs published on the
Kostyál page (DSC2362–DSC2753); captured in the image manifest.

**Notes.** The Kostyál page and the Dazed reference are image/JS-heavy; quoted
fragments are from the published gallery copy as rendered and via search
summaries. No "plunge pool" was confirmed in public sources (the brief mentions
it; treat as `needs_review`). No critical review located — coverage is
descriptive/press.
