# CUTE — Adventures in Symbiotic Love Tyranny

## Short description

Fornieles' specially commissioned work for the CUTE group show at Somerset House: a film, _Fini Season One: Adventures in Symbiotic Love Tyranny_ (dated 2023; shown 2024), set in his Fini / Finiliar universe. It features "an almost criminally chère chihuahua with a human voice" whose agenda "recapitulates the charismatic megafauna narrative (in this case, a mini-fauna): If we activate the cuteness center of your brain, you'll make room for us in your anthropic tyranny." The piece extends his Finiliar-era interest in cuteness as a tool of power, survival and capital. (Fact, per Spike/Apollo. Title corrected from earlier "Symbolic" to "Symbiotic.")

## Core question

How does cuteness operate as a covert mechanism of power, survival and control — rather than mere harmlessness — by activating a hard-wired human caregiving response? Can a non-human cute entity bargain its way into "your anthropic tyranny" precisely by being cute? (Fact + light inference.)

## Social machine

A film essay/narrative voiced by cute, non-human characters, embedded within a major thematic group exhibition (50+ artists, five themes: Cry Baby, Play Together, Monstrous Others, Sugar-Coated Pill, Hypersonic). The cuteness of the characters is itself the persuasion engine acting on the viewer. (Fact + inference.)

## Fictional device

A "futuristic, non-human cute entity" (the talking chihuahua of the Fini world) given voice and an explicit survival agenda — using its own cuteness as an argument addressed directly to the human viewer's neurology. The Fini/Finiliar conceit (cute creatures as living embodiments of data) supplies the fictional world. (Fact: Spike describes "futuristic, non-human cute entities whose survival depends on activating human emotional responses"; inference connecting to Fini lore.)

## Real-world system it attaches to

Kawaii / global cute consumer culture; the kawaii aesthetic's roots in the biological response to babies; NFT/crypto aesthetics and the Finiliar franchise; "charismatic megafauna" conservation-marketing logic; neoliberal capitalism (per the show's own curatorial claim that cuteness "thrives within the neoliberal capitalist structures which spawned it"). (Fact.)

## Audience / participant role

Gallery viewer positioned as the target of the cuteness operation: the film addresses the viewer's "cuteness center," asking them to notice their own involuntary caregiving/affection response being solicited and instrumentalised. (Inference, grounded in the work's stated address.)

## Performer / subject role

The cute non-human characters (chihuahua with a human voice) are both performers and pleaders — subjects arguing for their own survival. The human viewer's affect is the real material being performed upon. (Fact + inference.)

## Emotional engine

The involuntary "aww" reflex — the biological imperative to care triggered by baby-like features — deliberately activated and then made uneasy as the viewer realises the response is being used as leverage. Tenderness curdling into suspicion. (Inference from Fornieles' stated kawaii research + the film's agenda.)

## Ethical danger

The work performs the very manipulation it critiques: it uses cuteness to seduce while exposing seduction. Risk of the critique being absorbed as just more cute content (the show itself was a Sanrio/Hello-Kitty-50th, ticketed, Dr Jart+-sponsored spectacle), blunting the edge. The Apollo reviewer judged the show's subversion claim "more like wishful thinking than anything else." (Inference + fact on reception.)

## Aesthetic world

Tags: cuteness, kawaii, power, NFT aesthetics, film, capital, affect, control, charismatic megafauna, chihuahua, Fini, Finiliar, survival, anthropic tyranny, Roko's Basilisk (the reviewer's closing frame). Hyperpop / globalised-kawaii register of the wider show. (Fact + tags.)

## Research method

Builds on Fornieles' own research into "what elicits empathy within a viewer, tapping into the kawaii aesthetic, which comes from a biological connection to babies and a biological imperative to care," and on the Fini/Finiliar project (cute creatures as embodiments of live financial data, 2016–). (Fact.)

## Use of real material

Real neuroscience/ethology of the cuteness response; real kawaii/consumer-culture references; the real Fini/Finiliar franchise and its crypto-data lineage; the real exhibition context (Sanrio, Hello Kitty 50th). (Fact.)

## Use of fiction

A short fiction: a talking cute creature with a survival agenda, voicing in the first person the bargain cuteness strikes with human attention. Fiction is the delivery mechanism for an argument about affect and power. (Inference.)

## Emergent behaviour

The intended in-gallery effect — viewers catching their own affection reflex mid-firing and reinterpreting it as a transaction. Whether audiences read the critique or simply enjoyed the cuteness is the open question (and the show's central ambivalence per reviewers). (Inference.)

## Residue / documentation / afterlife

Shown 25 Jan – 14 Apr 2024, Embankment Galleries, Somerset House. Documented in reviews (Spike, Apollo, FAD; Evening Standard/Time Out/Guardian star reviews). Sits within the ongoing Fini/Finiliar body of work, so it has an afterlife in that franchise. One real film still catalogued in image_manifests (project: cute-somerset-house). (Fact.)

## What this project teaches the studio

That the studio can weaponise its own signature device (the cute Fini creature) reflexively — using cuteness to critique cuteness — and that placing such a work inside a mass-appeal, commercially-sponsored blockbuster both amplifies reach and risks defanging the critique. A lesson in critique-by-complicity. (Inference.)

## What failed or remained unresolved

Whether the critical edge survived the context: reviewers found the show's "cuteness undermines capitalism" thesis unconvincing/wishful. The film's specific narrative beats and length are not fully documented in public sources. (Fact on reception + gap.)

## Possible mutations

Further "Seasons" of Fini; pushing the survival-bargain conceit into interactive or LARP form; pairing the cute-affect critique with the Finiliar crypto-data mechanic more explicitly; a version sited outside the gallery/merch economy. (Inference / open.)
