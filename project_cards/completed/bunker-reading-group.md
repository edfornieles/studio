# & Bunker Reading Group (led by Ed Fornieles)

## Short description

A weekly online reading group led by Ed Fornieles (often co-hosted by Carlos/Ishikawa director Vanessa Carlos), run as part of the gallery's "weekly broadcast" programming during COVID-19 lockdown, beginning March 2020. Held Thursday evenings, 7pm GMT, on Zoom. Each week the group read/listened/watched assigned material — books, essays, podcasts, films — and discussed it from an explicitly personal perspective, asking how even abstract ideas or global events relate to participants' own pasts and daily lives.

## Core question

Can a reading group be a place to metabolise a global crisis personally — turning books and ideas into shared emotional and intellectual processing during lockdown?

## Social machine

A recurring Zoom reading group: assigned weekly material + a discussion protocol that insists on personal, lived interpretation, with rotating co-hosts and guests.

## Fictional device

Minimal/none — this is a real reading group rather than a fiction; the "device" is the framing protocol ("interpret whatever we are reading from a personal perspective"). (Fact.)

## Real-world system it attaches to

The COVID-19 lockdown; the commercial gallery (Carlos/Ishikawa) repurposed as a site of care and exchange; book clubs and group therapy; Zoom culture. (Fact.)

## Audience / participant role

Open participants who registered by email (gallery@carlosishikawa.com), read the material and shared personal reflections in discussion. (Fact.)

## Performer / subject role

Fornieles as host/facilitator; Vanessa Carlos co-hosting; invited guests (Tim Steer, Tamara Hart, Hamish MacPherson). Participants are subjects of their own reflection. (Fact.)

## Emotional engine

Loneliness, anxiety, trauma, love, heartbreak, grief and the need for connection under lockdown — the readings (trauma, attachment, love, anxiety, silence, time) directly name these. (Fact: reading list.)

## Ethical danger

The exposure of personal/emotional material in a semi-public group hosted by a gallery; boundaries between art-world programming, care, and therapy. (Inference.)

## Aesthetic world

Tags: Zoom, reading group, lockdown, care, emotional exchange, weekly broadcast, Carlos/Ishikawa, trauma, attachment, Afrofuturism.

## Research method

Curated reading list across psychology, attachment, love, anxiety, silence, time, and Afrofuturism; weekly facilitation; personal-interpretation protocol. (Fact.)

## Use of real material

Real books/essays/podcasts/films; real participants' lived experiences; the real conditions of lockdown. (Fact.)

## Use of fiction

Largely none — though fiction (Octavia Butler's Parable of the Sower, Friday Black, Afterglow) was used as reading material to think through reality. (Fact.)

## Emergent behaviour

A sustained community formed across ~a year of weekly sessions, with guests and shifting themes responding to the group's evolving needs. (Inference from the dated reading list.)

## Residue / documentation / afterlife

Documented on the Carlos/Ishikawa "weekly broadcast" page with a dated reading list (Mar 2020 – Mar 2021+). Connects to Fornieles' broader interest in care, emotion and attachment (cf. Finiliar, Associations, Cel). (Fact.)

## What this project teaches the studio

That a "social machine" need not be a fiction — a simple recurring protocol (read X, share personally) can build a real community of feeling, and that a studio/gallery can act as an infrastructure of care, not just display. (Inference.)

## What failed or remained unresolved

TKTK — interview Ed (how it ended; whether it fed directly into later emotional-systems works; the line between facilitation and therapy).

## Possible mutations

Ongoing or themed reading groups; reading groups attached to specific projects; a published reader/anthology of the list; in-person revivals. (Inference.)

## Venue / context

Online / Zoom — Carlos/Ishikawa "weekly broadcast" programming, London. 2020–2021 (began March 2020).
