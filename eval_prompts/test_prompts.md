# Test Prompts

Prompts for testing future Studio Clippy responses. Score responses with
`scoring_rubric.md`.

```text
I have an idea about an AI-run meaning camp. Interview me, then turn it into a project card.

Tell me where this idea repeats my old work too much.

Give me three mutations of Animal House for a post-AI context.

Use what you know about my emotional patterns, but do not therapise me. Help me understand why I am avoiding this project.

Take this loose idea and tell me: what is the social machine, what is the fictional device, and what would make it real?

Create three image-world suggestions for this project, but explain what each one does conceptually.

Turn this project into a one-page proposal.

What is the residue of this performance? What object, image, archive or sculpture remains?
```
