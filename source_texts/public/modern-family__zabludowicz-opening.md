# Modern Family — Ed Fornieles 'Modern Family' opens at Chisenhale Gallery (Zabludowicz Collection)
_source: https://www.zabludowiczcollection.com/news/view/ed-fornieles-modern-family-opens-at-chisenhale-gallery · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Date:** 18 September 2014
**Publication:** Zabludowicz Collection (news)

An LA- and London-based artist unveiled a significant new exhibition at Chisenhale Gallery in East London. The show, titled *Modern Family*, examines contemporary interpretations of the American "good life" and its idealised domestic spaces. The artist blends virtual and physical elements through "digital images, sculpture, choreographed performance and a new online commission." The Zabludowicz Collection provided partial financial support for this undertaking.

Exhibition details: ran from 19 September through 9 November 2014, with a preview event scheduled for 18 September from 6:30–8:30pm.
