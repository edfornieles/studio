# CUTE — Apollo + FAD Magazine (context, commission framing)

_sources: https://apollo-magazine.com/cute-somerset-house-review/ and https://fadmagazine.com/2024/01/31/meet-cute-at-somerset-house/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Apollo Magazine review:**
The exhibition features "specially commissioned pieces by artists including Ed Fornieles and Bart Seng Wen Long." (Confirms Fornieles' CUTE work was a new commission.)

Curatorial claim quoted by Apollo: "Cuteness thrives within the neoliberal capitalist structures which spawned it, while at the same time undermining their very foundations with its light-hearted affirmations of uncertainty." The Apollo reviewer is skeptical, calling this "more like wishful thinking than anything else."

**FAD Magazine (31 Jan 2024):**
Title given as "CUTE: An Exhibition Exploring the Irresistible Rise of Cuteness." Dates 25 January – 14 April 2024, Somerset House, The Strand. Sanrio involvement. Five themes: Cry Baby, Play Together, Monstrous Others, Sugar-Coated Pill, Hypersonic. Senior Curator Claire Catterall: "Cute is undeniably the most prominent aesthetic of our times. Yet, it has only now begun to be taken seriously"; its power has "slowly crept up on us." FAD highlights two commissions by name — Hannah Diamond's fruit-scented sleepover room and the Sin Wai Kin / Sian Fan interactive "Glimmer" — but does not detail Fornieles' contribution.
