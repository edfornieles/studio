# Crypto Certs — Cryptocurrency to fund an artist's long-term career (Happening.media)
_source: https://www.happening.media/category/magazine/fr/news/5c4718176a1dc012b0d1e201/ed-fornieles-overhauls-funding-models-in-new-cryptocurrency-art-project · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Author:** Henri Robert
**Date:** 22 January 2019
_(English rendering of the French-language article.)_

Ed Fornieles, an artist known for exploring tensions between virtual and physical worlds, launches a new venture. 'Crypto Certs' offers art investors a more active role in artwork production by revisiting traditional financing models. Long-term, Fornieles aims to convert more artists to this alternative approach.

Concerned with freeing conceptual artists from inherent career pressures—and their galleries—the project encourages collectors to invest in an artist's career rather than specific works. When purchasing a "Crypto Cert" (original prints by Fornieles), collectors pay €550 and receive both the artwork and a share of future profits. Inspired by Brad Troemel's bitcoin-incorporating sculpture, certificates come in two types:
- **Studio Certs** support the entire studio with progressive, sustainable returns on all production.
- **Project Certs** finance specific projects and grant holders percentage rights to project-related sales.

Generated funds support the artist's future development.

Each certificate uses Ethereum blockchain technology to store and distribute value. An embedded key grants owners rights to receive percentages from an Ethereum fund growing with each new certificate sale. Money withdrawal requires scratching the key area, damaging the certificate—raising whether profits ever exceed the artworks' value.

Fornieles states: "When the collector goes fuck the art," it becomes "purely speculative investment."

Fornieles believes this model will reduce art investment risks and encourage artistic boldness: "the current system rewards repeating past successes, so you end up making the same types of works."
