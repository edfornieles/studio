# Dorm Daze — xenogothic: "A Note on Ed Fornieles and Unconsciousness Raising"
_source: https://xenogothic.com/2019/08/02/a-note-on-ed-fornieles-and-unconsciousness-raising/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Author: xenogothic (Matt Colquhoun). Date: August 2, 2019.

On Dorm Daze: described as a "Facebook LARP" in which Fornieles "scraped the Facebook data of American college students to construct a community of fictitious characters before documenting their interactions together over a period of time and constructing a twisted coming-of-age drama out of the results."

On the later 72-hour performance Cel (for context on the lineage): participants "were invited to adopt[ed] hyper-aggressive patriarchal characters and archetypes drawn from personality profiles scraped from the likes of 4chan and Reddit" and would "embody these personas completely for the duration of their time enclosed in a cell constructed in Fornieles' studio." The work featured "a rigorous system of subtle communication for the giving and acknowledging of consent," and Fornieles described Cel as "a sort of inversion of 'second-wave feminist discourses'."

The author characterizes both works as "a kind of Artaudian consciousness raising session through which oppressions and violences were acknowledged and considered not through their open and collective discussion but through the open and collective performance of their affects."
