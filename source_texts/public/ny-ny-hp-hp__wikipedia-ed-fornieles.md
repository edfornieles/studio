# New York New York Happy Happy — Wikipedia: Ed Fornieles (NY NY HP HP section)
_source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Fornieles · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Year:** 2013 (the work is also catalogued as 2014 — see Carlos/Ishikawa & Meer caption).

**Description:** For "New York New York Happy Happy (NY NY HP HP)," Fornieles was invited by Rhizome to organize a gala at the New Museum, New York. The event was controversial because Fornieles scripted both actual Museum donors and hired actors, blurring any sense of reality between authentic patrons and performers.

**Mechanics (per Wikipedia / cited press):** guests adopted a heightened persona; participants were contacted by the artist in advance and given specific roles with cues to perform over the course of the evening. Roles included downtown art-world caricatures such as "E-Cig," "Art Talk," "Selfie," and most notably "Sociopath" / "a Patrick Bateman type." Actors with hidden agendas circulated among the guests. The project mapped narratives and character types defining the city's society life onto a social event, drawing on film and television — from Patrick Bateman to Woody Allen, Sex and the City to Girls.

**Context:** presented as a semi-fictional benefit by Rhizome, commissioned as part of the Performa Consortium (Performa 13), at the New Museum.

**Gallery caption (via Meer / Chisenhale):** "Ed Fornieles, New York New York Happy Happy (NY NY HP HP), 2014, Performance (in association with Rhizome), New Museum, New York. © the artist, courtesy Carlos/Ishikawa, London."
