# Jupiter Ascending — Wikipedia (Ed Fornieles)

_source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Fornieles · accessed 2026-06-10_

"With *Jupiter Ascending* (2015) at Carl Kostyal, Stockholm, Fornieles recreated a spa reflecting the meditative culture associated with care of the body." (Wikipedia, citing reference [13], linking to Dazed Digital.)

In the same article: "*Der Geist: Flesh Feast* (2016) at Arratia Beer in Berlin continued the body theme in which Fornieles expressed, through film, objects, and installation, his attempt to adopt a self-management diet." (Citing reference [14], ArtReview.)

_Note: The Wikipedia article dates Jupiter Ascending to 2015, consistent with the Kostyál exhibition page (19.02.2015–20.03.2015). The article does not mention the Bulletproof Diet or cartoon fox in relation to Jupiter Ascending — those belong to Der Geist._
