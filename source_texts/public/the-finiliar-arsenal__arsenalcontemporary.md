# The Finiliar (Arsenal) — Ed Fornieles | The Finiliar (Arsenal Contemporary Art, New York)
_source: https://www.arsenalcontemporary.com/ny/exhib/detail/the-finiliar-ed-fornieles · accessed 2026-06-10_

## Exhibition details
- Dates: **February 22 – April 23, 2017**. Opening reception Feb 22, 6–8 p.m.
- Location: **Arsenal Contemporary New York** — the gallery's inaugural exhibition. (21 Cortlandt Alley, 2nd Floor, New York, NY 10013.)
- Artist: Ed Fornieles (British, based in Montreal).
- Developed during a residency at **Arsenal Montréal** (the Finiliars series originated there, 2016).

## Works featured
- *Tulip Fever* (2017) — HD video, edition of 5, 9 min 21 s. (A single-channel video considering possible relations between Finiliars; dramas play out in an imagined corollary to each character's real-world trajectory; ran with three different soundtracks, shifting between explanation and advertisement.)
- Telematic LED works — three looming LED screens synced to real-time short- and long-term value projections.
- Inkjet prints (on dibond).
- Sculptures / merchandise-style objects.
- Animated digital video.

## Currencies referenced
Canadian dollar, British pound, Ethereum.

## Description / key quotes
> "Fornieles creates stages for performative cultures stemming from the ebb and flow of information through various systems of value."

> "Three looming LED screens synced to real-time short- and long-term value projections for the Canadian dollar, British pound, and Ethereum implicate the viewer in an empathetic relation to capital."

Finiliar defined (press release): "Digital entity feeding off data sets produced by companies, currencies, and other large institutions, using a cute facial interface to elicit a sense of emotional attachment within the viewer." The exhibition rebrands major currencies as characters inspired by Tamagotchi collectibles.
