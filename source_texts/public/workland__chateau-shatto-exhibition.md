**Title:** Workland: the fence is a narrow place
**Dates:** September 12 – November 14, 2015
**Venue:** Château Shatto, Los Angeles (downtown LA, Maple Avenue)
**Artists:** Ed Fornieles, with Hannah Black and Matt Goerzen

**Exhibition statement (verbatim excerpt):**

"This exhibition turns the gallery space into a communal workspace, a hive of individuals with self-ascribed tasks. You can watch people gently performing themselves working, not so different from the scene at your nearest café, where you may surround oneself with the atmosphere of productivity to help fuel your own."

**Featured works (as listed / described on the page):**

- Works by **Ed Fornieles**: digital prints with aluminum frames, polished aluminum constructions, video, and mixed-media installations. Named pieces include the "Concussion" series, "Black Mass" works, "The Man in the High Castle" (HD video, 1.57 min), "A Walk in the Woods" (coffee grounds, resin, painted steel), and "The Last Witch Hunter" (a soundproof-chamber installation). An installation-view image is also captioned "Maze Runner: The Scorch Trials." (Several of these titles are recent film/TV/streaming titles, repurposed as work titles.)
- Works by **Hannah Black**: steel and latex sculpture; video works including "Positive Visualization 1."
- Works by **Matt Goerzen**: security-themed adhesive vinyl prints and posters ("security decals").

**Contact on page:** gallery@chateaushatto.com (subject: "Inquiry | Workland: the fence is a narrow place").
