# The Dreamy Awards — AnOther Magazine: "Ed Fornieles & The Dreamy Awards"
_source: https://www.anothermag.com/art-photography/2185/ed-fornieles-the-dreamy-awards · accessed 2026-06-10_

> Note: copyright-protected body; detailed extract with short attributed quotations.

**Event:** The Dreamy Awards, 7 September 2012, Serpentine Gallery Park Nights, Hyde Park. An immersive performance artwork recognizing "those individuals and institutions that are helping to shape our current cultural and technological landscape."

**Participant structure (three tiers):** approximately 30% were real public figures from "music, technology, advertising, film, TV, politics"; the remaining ~70% were members of the public assigned character roles created by Fornieles.

**Mechanics:** participants received "plot points" and character profiles to guide their interactions. Fornieles stressed flexibility:
> "your profile is just a starting point, and the plot points are just a starting point—there should be a feeling that you can shift and change when you want to."

**Artist intent:** Fornieles was interested in award ceremonies as celebrations that "reveal the priorities of the people doing the celebrating." He admitted the selection was deliberately biased:
> "The real reason why many of those people are winning those awards is because I like them."

**Outcome:** the event was designed to generate "a massive wealth of information and material," resulting in a film work for the BBC's iPlayer channel plus sculptural and video installations.
