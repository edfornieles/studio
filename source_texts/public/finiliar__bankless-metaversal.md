# Finiliar — Discover new NFTs: Finiliar (Bankless / Metaversal, William M. Peaster)
_source: https://metaversal.banklesshq.com/p/discover-new-nfts-finiliar · accessed 2026-06-10_

## Core mechanics
Finiliar is a collection of **10,000 ERC-721 NFTs** featuring dynamic creature characters linked via oracles to cryptocurrency price movements. Each finiliar connects to one of ten cryptocurrencies: ETH, BTC, BNB, SOL, AVAX, DOGE, MATIC, LINK, UNI, or XTZ.

## Mood system & update intervals
The creatures exhibit emotional states that shift based on their linked crypto's performance. Update frequencies vary by rarity:
- Hourly (most common)
- Daily
- Twice daily
- Weekly
- Monthly (rarest)

> "finilar are living NFTs … They become happy and buoyant when the currency they are linked to does well, and sad or even sick when it performs poorly."

## Timeline & sales
- **January 2022**: pre-sale of 1,000 eggs at 0.06 ETH each.
- **March 2022**: main sale of 8,790 remaining eggs at 0.1 ETH each (20 eggs per transaction limit).
- **Q2 2022**: physical and emotional animations rollout.
- Floor price at time of writing: 0.22 ETH (~30 eggs listed).
