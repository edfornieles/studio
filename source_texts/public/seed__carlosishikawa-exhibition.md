# Seed — Carlos/Ishikawa exhibition page (checklist)
_source: https://www.carlosishikawa.com/exhibitions/seed/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Ed Fornieles, "Seed"
22 September – 28 October 2017 (private view 21 September, 6–9pm).
Carlos/Ishikawa, Unit 4, 88 Mile End Road, London E1 4UN.

The gallery page presents installation and detail views with a works checklist (no extended press-release prose on the page itself; the curatorial statements are in the downloadable booklet — see seed__carlosishikawa-booklet.md).

## Works checklist (titles, media, dimensions — all 2017)

1. **Sim Vol.1** (2017) — Installation
2. **Test Studies** (2017) — 18:17 min looped HD video
3. **Sim Vol.1: Existential Risk** (2017) — A4 book, 30 × 21 cm
4. **Mii Friends** (2017) — Digital print on foam board, 200 × 220 × 133 cm
5. **Mii Friends Conversation Reports** (2017) — Inkjet on paper, 233 × 246 cm
6. **Finiliar** (2017) — Installation
7. **Tulip Fever** (2017) — 9:21 min looped video animation
8. **The Truth Table Experience** (2017) — Virtual reality set and bed, 190 × 90 × 50 cm
9. **Test combination I & II** (2017) — Magnets on magnetic whiteboard, 120 × 180 cm each
10. **Truth Table** (2017) — Installation
11. **Babble Box** (2017) — Extruded aluminium, acrylic panels, audio equipment, 105 × 56 × 44 cm
