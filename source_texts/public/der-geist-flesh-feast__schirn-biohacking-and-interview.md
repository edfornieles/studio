**"The world of bio-hacking" (Schirn Double Feature, screened 28 Sep 2016).** *Der Geist – Flesh Feast* (2016) is an eight-minute video, part of an installation shown in Berlin. The work features a man's voice belonging to a fox character — Fornieles' alter-ego — recounting experiences on the journey toward self-optimization: memory exercises and workouts meant to sharpen the mind, leading to the "recoding of one's own identity." The work references self-optimization diets, with the fox exploring product presentations by Dave Asprey and starter diet packs that promise a journey toward a new self. Dave Asprey, inventor of the "Bulletproof Diet," promotes a "high-performance life" through a "hack" in human biology.

**"Upgrade your mind" interview (19 Apr 2016).** Framed around "control mania" in self-management cultures and Fornieles' "Bio Hacking Nail Art" performance. Fornieles explains that new-age lifestyles and diets stem from systems approaching the individual and the body, and that dieting represents the pinnacle of self-management — a process where the body and mind become something to be engineered toward specific goals.
