# Finiliar — Culture Crossover: Finiliar by Ed Fornieles turns data into empathy tool (Computerworld)
_source: https://www.computerworld.com/article/1627715/culture-crossover-finiliar-by-ed-fornieles-turns-data-into-empathy-tool.html · accessed 2026-06-10_

## Key quotes from Ed Fornieles
On a specific creature: "This one is attached to the stocks of Facebook. And it's very happy at the moment."

On broader applications: "This could also be attached to carbon emissions. It could be attached to the rainforest. And it is a societal responsibility to change the Faniliar, essentially."

On the purpose: "The Finiliar is an attempt to try to give a body to infrastructure or to things that normally don't have an existence in a reality."

On human agency: "I think that our agency within the eye of the storm is miniscule and it's immersive. But we do have some agency."

## Mechanics
The digital creature's emotional state fluctuates with real-time market data — soaring shares produce happiness; plunging shares create sadness and eventual sickness. The work demonstrates how individual emotional responses have limited power; only collective group reactions can alter the system's trajectory.

## Additional context
Fornieles created an animated film titled *Tulip Fever* exploring these themes. The work was launched/shown on the Malevich trading platform.
