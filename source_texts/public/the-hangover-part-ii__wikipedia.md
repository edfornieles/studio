# The Hangover Part II — Wikipedia (Ed Fornieles)
_source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Fornieles · accessed 2026-06-10_

On Dorm Daze: "Dorm Daze, grew out of Fornieles's interest in American fraternity culture as a large-scale on-line performance played-out on Facebook, where participants adopted profiles taken from real life American college students."

On The Hangover Part II: "Ed Fornieles first solo gallery exhibition, The Hangover Part II, constructed from the detritus of Animal House, was organized at the Carlos/Ishikawa Gallery, London in 2011."

On Despicable Me 2: "Despicable Me 2 (2013) at the Mihai Nicodim Gallery in Los Angeles, invented a female character, 'Britney Rivers' who develops a stereotypical personality as she builds her network of friends on Social Media."

Biographical note (Carlos/Ishikawa artist page): Born 1983 in the UK; lives and works in London. MA in Sculpture, Royal College of Art, 2011; BA Fine Art, Ruskin School, Oxford, 2005. (Note: the Nicodim press release gives b. 1986; date of birth conflicts across sources.)
