# Finiliar Land — Neotopia: Data and Humanity (Art Center Nabi)
_source: https://nabi.or.kr/en/page/board_view.php?brd_idx=795&brd_id=project · accessed 2026-06-10_

## Exhibition overview
"The artworks utilizing 'data' from various fields of the society, culture, politics, economics present the 'more humane society'."

## Dates & venues
- **Art Center Nabi** (Seorin-dong, Jongro-gu): November 1, 2017 – January 31, 2018.
- Nabi Future Studies (Tongui-dong, Jongno-gu).
- Tazak Madang (Jangchung-dong, Jung-gu).
- **COMO** (Eulji-ro, Jung-gu, SK-T tower): January 15 – February 28, 2018.

## Sponsors
Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, Japan Foundation Seoul, Goethe-Institut Korea.

## Ed Fornieles contribution
- Title: **_Finiliar Land_ (2017)**.
- Location: COMO exhibition space.
