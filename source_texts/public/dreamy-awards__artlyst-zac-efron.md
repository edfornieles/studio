# The Dreamy Awards — Artlyst: "Zac Efron To Appear At Serpentine Gallery Dreamy Awards"
_source: https://artlyst.com/news/zac-efron-to-appear-at-serpentine-gallery-dreamy-awards/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

> Note: copyright-protected body; detailed extract with short attributed quotations. (Article publication date ~29 August 2012; event date 7 September 2012.)

**Zac Efron's role:** the Hollywood actor would "accept his Dreamy Award onscreen" during the evening event in the Serpentine Gallery Pavilion.

**Award categories:** the Dreamy winners represent "the worlds of music, television, politics, business, technology and film." (No individual honorees beyond Efron are listed.)

**Micro-society / mechanic:** designed as a participatory experience where "participants will be encouraged to assume a new identity for the evening," with character options ranging "from princess to tech legend; from political spin-doctor to superstar DJ."

**Artistic intent:** Fornieles' work explores character construction, aiming to create "a situation where social equality is forged through the shared experience of adopting a new character."

**Documentation:** a film of the event would become artwork featured on The Space (Arts Council England / BBC digital initiative).
