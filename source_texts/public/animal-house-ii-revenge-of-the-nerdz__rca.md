# Animal House II: Revenge of the Nerdz — source text (RCA)
_source: Carlos/Ishikawa CV; Dazed "Animal House" feature (2011); Wikipedia/Grokipedia · accessed 2026-06-10_

Animal House II: Revenge of the Nerdz (2011) — Royal College of Art, London. The sequel/second iteration of Fornieles' Animal House performance, staged during/around his MA Sculpture at the RCA (completed 2011).

Context from the original Animal House (Guest Projects, London Fields, 17 January 2011):
- 120 invited guests, 12 actors, 5 camera crews.
- Attendees received character descriptions via Facebook RSVP and performed actions sourced from college blogs, student Facebook profiles, and frat-house films (Animal House, Sorority Wars, Road Trip).
- Sculptural sets: a bed, a white clapboard house with red door, a beer-filled fish tank.
- Participants in college sweaters performed scripted scenes; "Shout performance" referenced the Animal House film.
- "Revenge of the Nerdz" is the RCA-staged second instalment continuing the immersive American-college-party simulation.
