# Seed — The Seen: "Ed Fornieles: The Finiliar" (Elliot Mickleburgh)
_source: http://theseenjournal.org/art-seen-national/ed-fornieles-finilar/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Author: Elliot Mickleburgh. Publication: THE SEEN. Date: 27 April 2017.
Exhibition reviewed: Ed Fornieles: The Finiliar at Arsenal Contemporary, New York (through 23 April 2017) — the sibling/precursor presentation of the Finiliar body of work that also appeared in Seed at Carlos/Ishikawa.

Summary (paraphrase): The review examines Fornieles' exhibition featuring the "Finiliar" — digital entities designed to visualise market data through cute animated characters. The artist defines a Finiliar as a "digital entity feeding off data sets produced by companies, currencies, and other large institutions."

Mickleburgh notes the exhibition functions like a technology trade show, with merchandise and LED monitors displaying animated Finiliars reacting to real-time currency and cryptocurrency fluctuations (Canadian dollar, British Pound Sterling, Ethereum).

The critic argues the work's significance lies in its subversion of data utility: rather than providing meaningful market analysis, it prioritises "instant aesthetic gratification." This approach creates emotional engagement — when a Finiliar weeps as Ethereum value drops, viewers experience empathy, prompting reflection on how global economic fluctuations affect human lives.
