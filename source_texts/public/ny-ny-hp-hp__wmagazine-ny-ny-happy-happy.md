**Publication:** W Magazine. **Author:** Fan Zhong. **Date:** 19 November 2013. **Category:** Art & Design.

**Event overview:** British artist Ed Fornieles' performance piece "New York New York Happy Happy," presented as a "semi-fictional benefit" during Performa 13 at the New Museum. The event was ostensibly a Rhizome charity gala.

**Scripting / mechanics:** Fornieles incorporated subversive elements including actors with undisclosed agendas seeded among the guests, and asked attendees to perform cued actions (e.g. holding hands). The piece blended the traditional charity-gala format with a scripted layer of concealed performers interacting with genuine attendees.

**Escalation:** Midway through the evening, Chippendale-style dancers disrupted proceedings, removing clothing from themselves and from guests; the event "quickly unraveled" into chaos.

**Quoted reactions:**
- One attendee: "He undid my bra from inside my top! So I had to give it to him!" (on a dancer's antics).
- New Museum director Lisa Phillips, surprised: "But did you see that man take his pants off in front of me?"
