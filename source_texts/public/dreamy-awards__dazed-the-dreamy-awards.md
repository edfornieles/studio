# The Dreamy Awards — Dazed: "The Dreamy Awards"
_source: http://www.dazeddigital.com/artsandculture/article/14415/1/the-dreamy-awards · accessed 2026-06-10_

> Note: copyright-protected body; detailed extract with short attributed quotations.

**Event:** awards ceremony at the Serpentine Gallery Pavilion, 7 September 2012. Approximately 230 guests attended, including cast members from *Made in Chelsea* and figures from music, fashion, film, television and media.

**Character assignment / structure:** participants received detailed character profiles with specific behavioral guidelines, sartorial choices, and five predetermined plot points to execute during the evening. Fornieles:
> "The process of character assigning tries to give the individual something that is far enough away from their personality for them to make distinct decisions."

**Zac Efron as honoree:** Efron received the primary award. Fornieles:
> "the Disney Corporation spent millions of pounds on him and his image, and what he's trying to do at the moment is trying to translate all those Disney dollars into his own, into something different."

**Artistic goal — empathy through role-play:** Fornieles sought social structures where people might experience empathy through role-play:
> "you start using some of the processes of getting into character then you have the potential to turn left where you'd normally turn right."

**Collaboration / documentation:** presented in Serpentine's Park Nights; pavilion designed by Herzog & de Meuron with Ai Weiwei; documentation later appeared on The Space (Arts Council England / BBC digital initiative).
