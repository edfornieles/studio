# Characterdate (Frame, Frieze London) — source text
_source: Carlos/Ishikawa CV; LEAP "Five Emerging Artists" (2012); Daisy Hogan bio (Tumblr); Wikipedia · accessed 2026-06-10_

Characterdate (2012) — presented by Carlos/Ishikawa in the Frame section of Frieze Art Fair London, October 2012.

- Described as a "semi-fictional dating network."
- Sculptural installation continuing Fornieles' interest in American college culture / role-play.
- Presented at the Carlos/Ishikawa booth.
- Daisy Hogan worked as assistant and casting coordinator for CharacterDate at the Carlos/Ishikawa Booth during Frieze 2012.
- Wikipedia lists it as "Characterdate, Frame (2012) at Frieze Art Fair London … continued exploring American college culture through sculptural installation."
