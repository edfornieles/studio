# Ed Fornieles — Biography (Carlos/Ishikawa gallery CV)
_source: https://www.carlosishikawa.com/artists/edfornieles/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**b. 1983, UK; Lives and works in London**

## Education
- 2011 — MA Sculpture, Royal College of Art, London, UK
- 2005 — BA Fine Art, Ruskin School of Drawing and Fine Art, Oxford University, UK
- 2003 — Cunliffe Prize, judged by Anya Gallaccio
- 2001 — Winchester University, HND Art & Design, UK

## Selected Solo Exhibitions & Performances
- 2024 — *Inside Out 2*, Carlos/Ishikawa, London
- 2021 — *Associations*, HeK (Haus der elektronischen Künste), Basel (Online); *Associations*, Carlos/Ishikawa, London
- 2019 — *Cel*, Center For Contemporary Art Futura, Prague; *Cel*, Carlos/Ishikawa, London
- 2017 — *Seed*, Carlos/Ishikawa, London; *The Finiliar*, Arsenal, New York; *Sim Vol. 1*, Alyssa Davis Gallery, New York
- 2016 — *Truth Table*, Cura - Basement Roma, Rome; *Der Geist: Flesh Feast*, Arratia Beer, Berlin
- 2015 — *Workland: the fence is a narrow place*, Chateau Shatto, Los Angeles; *Jupiter Ascending*, Carl Kostyal, Stockholm
- 2014 — *Modern Family*, Chisenhale Gallery, London
- 2013 — *New York New York Happy Happy*, New Museum, New York; *Pool Party Plays Itself*, MOCAtv, LA; *Despicable Me 2*, Mihai Nicodim, Los Angeles
- 2012 — *The Dreamy Awards*, Park Nights, Serpentine Gallery, London; *Characterdate*, Frame, Frieze Art Fair, London; *Happy Days in Basel*, Theater Basel
- 2011 — *The Hangover Part II*, Carlos/Ishikawa, London; *Animal House II: Revenge of the Nerdz*, Royal College of Art, London; *Animal House*, Guest Projects, London
