# Truth Table — Ed Fornieles: Truth Table (CURA. Magazine)
_source: https://curamagazine.com/exhibitions/ed-fornieles-truth-table/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Title:** Truth Table
**Venue:** Basement Roma (Viale Mazzini 128, 00195 Rome)
**Dates:** October 26 – December 23, 2016
**Text:** Dorothy Howard. **Institution:** CURA. Magazine / Basement Roma.

## Full text (as captured)

This marked Fornieles' inaugural solo show in Italy. The Montreal-based artist
converted the gallery into an immersive experience featuring a VR product where
viewers inhabited different bodies engaged in sexual interactions. The
installation employed randomized algorithms that continuously shifted body
configurations and viewer perspectives.

The gallery is converted into the provider of an experience: "a new VR product
that allows the viewer to occupy a changing host of bodies as they engage in a
chain of sexual interactions." Every roughly 20 seconds, the bodies, sexual
positions, and environments change, with variables such as race, body type, and
age equally represented.

The work deliberately countered social-media filter bubbles by using equal
distribution of variables rather than preference-reinforcing algorithms. "Each
type and form is given equal emphasis," creating "jarring" moments that strip
away familiar patterns. The use of randomization "pushes against the
re-enforcing bubble; instead, equally distributed variables work to counteract
expectations and predilections."

The exhibition functioned similarly to dream states — disjointed scenarios
implicating viewers while advancing Fornieles' exploration of "algorithmic
content models, immersive virtual realities, and technologies of sexuality." The
simulation "negotiates the psychosomatic forms of relationality implied by
algorithmic content models, immersive virtual realities, and technologies of
sexuality."
