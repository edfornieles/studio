# Modern Family — Ed Fornieles. Modern Family (Meer / Chisenhale press release)
_source: https://www.meer.com/en/10561-ed-fornieles-modern-family · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Exhibition:** 19 September – 9 November 2014, Chisenhale Gallery, London

Chisenhale Gallery presented Fornieles's first major UK solo show, described as exploring how the material logic of home improvement, sitcom architecture and the shopping mall creates a distorted "Pinterest reality."

The installation combined multiple elements:
- A new website aggregating live social-media content (Tumblr, Instagram, Twitter, Google feeds).
- Sculptural works mixing high-spec finishes and new technology with breakfast cereal and DIY home tiling.
- Performance pieces with choreographed movements based on lifestyle-based behaviours.

Fornieles examined the family unit as "a device for the replication and perpetuation of social norms" and explored "the collapse between online and offline spaces." The commissioned website used search terms to draw real-time data, utilising freely available personal and public data feeding our attention economy, where value is determined by likes, shares and reposts.

Artist background: Born 1983 in Petersfield, Fornieles works between Los Angeles and London. Previous projects included *Dorm Daze* (2011) and *Maybe New Friends* (2013).
