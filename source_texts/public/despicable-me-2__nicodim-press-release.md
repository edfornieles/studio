# Despicable Me 2 — Nicodim Gallery exhibition / press release
_source: https://www.nicodimgallery.com/exhibitions/ed-fornieles-despicable-me-2 · accessed 2026-06-10_

Ed Fornieles: DESPICABLE ME 2
Mihai Nicodim Gallery, Los Angeles
July 13 – August 10, 2013

Press release (key excerpts):

The exhibition is Fornieles's "first US solo exhibition" as a British artist working with internet and social media platforms.

The artist employs "a reality TV format to generate a new body of work following Britney Rivers as she navigates her way online: making friends, gaining likes and becoming the star of her own online reality."

Fornieles's installation "functions like a set wherein objecthood and value-systems are in flux," with objects serving as performative nodes connecting to broader networks of references and experiences.
