# Cursed Images — Galerie Kandlhofer exhibition overview
_source: https://www.kandlhofer.com/exhibitions/79/overview/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Title:** Cursed Images (curated by Ed Fornieles)
**Dates:** 17 September – 11 October 2019
**Venue:** Galerie Kandlhofer (Galerie Lisa Kandlhofer), Vienna
**Context:** Part of the "curated by" festival (curated_by 2019, theme "Circulation"), Vienna's gallery-wide guest-curator festival.
**Role:** Fornieles as **curator** (group exhibition), not as exhibiting artist.

**Participating artists:**
John Bock, Doug Bressler, Chris Burden, VALIE EXPORT, Ann Hirsch, Joey Holder, Rachel Maclean, Otto Muehl, Naked, Hermann Nitsch, Puppies Puppies, Rudolf Schwarzkogler, Omsk Social Club, Tommy Turner, Anna Uddenberg, David Wojnarowicz, Issy Wood, Katerina Zbortkova.

**Curatorial statement by Ed Fornieles (as reported on the page):**

Fornieles describes having spent years collecting and spreading "cursed images" — images containing "a demonic spirit that inhabits certain images and which passes to the viewer on contact, unsettling them, causing confusion, unease or dread."

The exhibition references the 2015 Tumblr account cursedimages.tumblr.com, which began to define and categorize images that produce "a disruptive and unsettling effect on the viewer." Such images share a quality of appearing "illogical, abnormal or wrong in some fundamental way," not tied to any specific content or subject matter.

He invokes Freud's concept of the **uncanny** — "a strangeness in the ordinary" — and references **Lacan's** broader definition, positioning the curse as placing the viewer "in the field where we do not know how to distinguish bad and good, pleasure from displeasure."

Two key moves in the statement:
1. The curse functions as **a cure** — "allowing us to see the borders of our expectations, a hidden desire for harmony."
2. Sustained exposure produces a second stage in which "the curse bleeds out into your everyday world," and "inanimate objects begin to develop their own agency."
