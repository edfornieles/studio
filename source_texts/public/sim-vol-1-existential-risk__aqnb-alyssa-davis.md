## Captured / synthesized

"SIM Vol. 1" was on view at Alyssa Davis Gallery, New York, opening May 19, 2017.
Curated by Ilya Smirnov (artist/curator, co-founder of Alyssa Davis Gallery),
with concept by Fornieles.

The exhibition took its starting point from a Google Hangout that took place in
February, with four players named **Harrie, Sasha, Jesse and Rebecca**, who
communicated from **Middelburg, the Netherlands**, in conversation with a
Montréal-based Project Manager (Fornieles).

The players went through an in-depth profile-creation process to "place emphasis
on the players being themselves." Their avatars were guided by their own
"limitations, strengths and moral code" through scenarios proposed by the
Project Manager.

Fornieles presents a display case containing the so-called **Game Book** — a
manual to the game — opened on a double page containing the subheadings
"Emotional Confrontation," "A Last Walk," "No Escape" and "Death as Gateway," as
well as an illustration titled "Multidimensional Being."

The narratives revolve around threats — some repelled, others unstoppable — with
3D animations illustrating players' reports about illness spreading and people
dying.
