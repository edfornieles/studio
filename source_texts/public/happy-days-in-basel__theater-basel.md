# Happy Days in Basel — source text (Theater Basel)
_source: Theater Basel Saisonvorschau 2012/2013 (designed by Ludovic Balland); WebSearch summaries; Carlos/Ishikawa CV · accessed 2026-06-10_

Happy Days in Basel (2012) — a Facebook sitcom edited by Ed Fornieles and Max Luz, commissioned around Theater Basel.

- The theatre season ran for one season (~10 months, September 2012 – June 2013); the Facebook sitcom ran for only ~3 weeks.
- The Theater Basel season-preview publication invited readers to follow the theatre's content and the Facebook sitcom in parallel, "which could lead to surprising collisions."
- Theatre pages were descriptive and information-focused; the Facebook sitcom mixed fiction and reality.
- Extends Fornieles' Facebook-performance method (Dorm Daze) into an institutional theatre context.
