## Speakers & backgrounds
- **Ed Fornieles**: fine artist; MA in Sculpture from the Royal College of Art; former technical assistant to Anish Kapoor; created Finiliar initially as gallery-exhibited artwork.
- **Sam Spike**: artistic director at Fingerprints DAO; collaborated on the NFT of Tim Berners-Lee's original World Wide Web source code (Sotheby's, 2021).
- **Jake Allen**: former Head of Product at Cameo (early employee, ~10th person); joined crypto/Web3 ~2–3 years before recording.

## Project overview
Core concept: "adorable digital creatures or digital animals, whose moods and whose actions are tied to different sources of data, like real time data feeds. Specifically, financial asset prices."

Origin: Ed conceived the project ~6–7 years prior as conceptual art exploring "how to foster some kind of empathy between people and these sort of vast, abstract, usually extremely impersonal sets of data."

Evolution: started as gallery video work (2016–2017) with monochromatic, simple blob-like forms; gallery sculptures created alongside digital versions; in 2022 launched as a 10,000-token NFT collection.

## Technical structure & mechanics
- 10 crypto families/assets (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Dogecoin, Avalanche, Tezos, etc.); mood/health driven by price action of corresponding cryptocurrencies.
- "you can now train your Finnie's to different assets."
- 150 different clans (Pokédex-style classification); 11,000+ individual traits across the collection; 1,000+ unique handmade textures.
- Invisible rarity system: variations not indexed in metadata, discoverable only visually.
- Funding model: pre-launch of ~1,000 eggs funded the year-long 3D-modeling production before the full 10,000 launch.

## Design & philosophy
- Anthropomorphization to make abstract financial forces emotionally legible. Ed: "what is interesting is when these structures facilitate different types of production that could otherwise never be imagined."
- Humor: Sam notes "there's humor in the Finneys themselves" — e.g., Dogecoin rendered as a Victorian dog society, plus characters like "Psycho" (a serial-killer dog) with murder victims.
- "bodies and faces are the original interfaces... Finnie's are evolving, quite literally evolving."
- Concept draws on Philip Pullman's *His Dark Materials* (familiars/dæmons as "both part of them, but also somehow separate"), creating tools for personal self-reflection and relationship-building.

## iOS app & future directions
- Current phase (at recording): TestFlight beta with financial tracking (CoinGecko-equivalent functionality).
- Planned: health/fitness data integration (step tracking, exercise rings); personal-data connectivity beyond crypto prices; naming functionality (not yet enabled); social/multiplayer interactions.
