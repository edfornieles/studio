# Cel — Time Out London review (Eddy Frankel)
_source: https://www.timeout.com/london/art/ed-fornieles-cel · accessed 2026-06-10_

Rating: 4 out of 5 stars. Critic: Eddy Frankel, Art Editor (UK). Published Wednesday 27 March 2019. Categories: Art, Contemporary art.

Summary (paraphrase of the review): The exhibition explores incel culture through a performance-based artwork. Fornieles created a role-playing game where participants lived in a house for two days, filmed via chest cameras and CCTV. The piece documents hierarchical dynamics that escalate into increasingly aggressive behaviour — including forced shaving, physical violence, and humiliation — before participants recognise the underlying causes of their anger rooted in societal neglect or abuse.

Frankel notes the work sits "halfway between something like the Stanford Prison Experiment and a particularly nasty episode of 'Big Brother'." The grainy, rough aesthetic mirrors YouTube street-fight footage.

The critic acknowledges the video runs somewhat long and suggests the artist's attempt to explain incel psychology feels "a little over-egged." However, the review concludes that viewers leave "deeply aware that male anger and aggression exist" and that such dynamics merit serious confrontation — "not a situation that's going to change, but it can — and should — be confronted."
