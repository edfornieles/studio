Pool Party Plays Itself (2013) — a performance and video piece by Ed Fornieles produced in conjunction with MOCAtv, Los Angeles.

"Pool Party is a film by Ed Fornieles that portrays a tapestry of archetypal Hollywood characters, scenarios, and mythologies in a condensed reality-television format, set in an upscale Los Angeles bungalow where twenty-five actors in a seamless role-play act out an escalating sequence of events drawn from pop culture sources including dating contest shows, celebrity lifestyle programming, cautionary tales from the film industry, and teen vampire dramas."
