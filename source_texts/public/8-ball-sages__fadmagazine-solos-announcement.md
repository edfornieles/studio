# 8 Ball Sages — Ed Fornieles' new series coming this September from SOLOS (FAD Magazine)
_source: https://fadmagazine.com/2024/07/29/ed-fornieles-new-series-coming-this-september-from-solos/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Author:** Mark Westall
**Date:** 29 July 2024

SOLOS has announced that their upcoming September release will be a new NFT Art series by British artist Ed Fornieles. This release was curated by Nico Epstein, an art historian and contemporary art advisor.

## THE WORK

The 8 Ball Sages are a cast of responsive, uniquely animated characters. External factors determine each Sage's mood and charm. Ownership involves stewarding an artwork—essentially caring for "a dependable AI-based being, a futuristic digital companion."

This presentation honors the quintessential analogue toy while gesturing toward "a darker disembodied future of interactivity where our conversations with machines bring us further away from interpersonal intimacy."

Originally released in the 1940s, the Magic 8 Ball functions as a fortune-telling device. Today, artificial intelligence serves a similar cultural role. Fornieles' creatures highlight AI's playful aspects while questioning its potential darker applications.

The Sages draw inspiration from Zoltan fun-park fortune tellers and the 2001 film A.I. Artificial Intelligence. They reflect a nostalgic era when technological wisdom felt whimsical rather than concerning. However, their existence depends on technology—blockchain disbursement, web hosting, and code.

These works continue Fornieles' investigation into consumerism and capitalist cultures through dynamic visual data representations. Recent projects include the relationship app Oom and Finiliars, cartoon characters whose behaviors fluctuate with currency markets. Fornieles exhibited recently at Carlos/Ishikawa in London and will launch new artwork with Hyundai Art Lab.

## THE RELEASE

The 8 Ball Sages release comprises 500 digital artworks. A live minting event for the first 100 works occurs Friday, September 13th, 6-8 pm at Shoreditch Arts Club in London.

## About the Artist

Ed Fornieles (b.1983) is a London-based artist known for innovative, interdisciplinary contemporary art. A Royal College of Art graduate, his work spans sculpture, installation, and digital media, exploring social behavior, technology, and data manipulation. Recent exhibitions include Inside Out 2 at Carlos/Ishikawa, London; Cute, Somerset House, London; Worldbuilding: Gaming and Art in the Digital Age, Centre Pompidou-Metz; Julia Stoschek Collection, Düsseldorf; Cel, Center For Contemporary Art Futura, Prague; The Finiliar, Arsenal, New York.

SOLOS is a gallery program exploring curation in the digital age. The platform believes "the art world has evolved beyond traditional galleries and museums." It collaborates with artists whose work showcases technology's influence on contemporary society. The gallery emphasizes that "art doesn't need to exist solely in a physical form; its essence lies in the concept and creative process." Digital artworks in their native medium (NFTs) represent the truest format.
