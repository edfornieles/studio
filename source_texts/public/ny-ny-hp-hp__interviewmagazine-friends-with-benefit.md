# New York New York Happy Happy — Interview Magazine: "Ed Fornieles' Friends, With Benefit"
_source: https://www.interviewmagazine.com/art/ed-fornieles-new-york-new-york-happy-happy · accessed 2026-06-10_

> Note: Interview Magazine's full body text is copyright-protected; the publisher's fetcher declined verbatim reproduction. The following is a detailed extract of the article's facts and short attributed quotations.

**Publication:** Interview Magazine, 11 November 2013. **Author:** Kurt McVey. **Event:** performance art gala at The Sky Room, The New Museum, New York.

**Framing:** The article describes Ed Fornieles' conceptual art gala "New York New York Happy Happy," presented by the performance-art biennial Performa 13 in conjunction with Rhizome and The New Museum.

**Persona-assignment mechanics:** Fornieles invited guests to embody specific character roles drawn from downtown art-world archetypes, including "E-Cig," "Art Talk," and "Selfie." The author was assigned "Sociopath" — described as "a Patrick Bateman type."

**Notable attendees named in the piece:**
- Hari Nef (performance artist; the writer likened her to the Childlike Empress from *The Neverending Story*)
- Genevieve Belleveau (Brooklyn contemporary art figure)
- Labanna Babylon (YouTube creator)
- Contessa Stuto (described as "rapping art party matron")

**Performance elements described:** artists on stretchers covered in sliced meat served as edible garnish; models in Miley Cyrus–inspired outfits; male strippers; a concluding "pillow fight" that escalated into theatrical chaos. The body was treated as a living canvas — Fornieles himself appeared in "cascading purple Yves Klein paint gashes" across a white suit and black boots.

**Theme:** downtown New York's influence on pop culture, staged through scripted social ritual that blended performance art, music, dance and social commentary.
