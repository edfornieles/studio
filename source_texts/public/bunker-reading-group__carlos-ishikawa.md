# & Bunker Reading Group (led by Ed Fornieles) — source text
_source: https://www.carlosishikawa.com/exhibitions/weeklybroadcast/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

A weekly online reading group hosted by Carlos/Ishikawa as part of its "weekly broadcast" programming during COVID-19 lockdown, led by Ed Fornieles (co-hosted at times by gallery director Vanessa Carlos). Began March 2020.

## Format
- Thursday evenings, 7pm GMT, on Zoom. Register by emailing gallery@carlosishikawa.com.
- Participants read/listened/watched assigned material (books, essays, podcasts, films) and discussed it from a personal perspective — "how even abstract ideas or global events might relate to our pasts and day to day lives."
- Gallery framing: maintaining "a place of emotional and intellectual exchange" during lockdown; "we need artists to give language and shape to complex realities and experiences."
