# Finiliar — Who Created Fini NFTs? Meet Ed Fornieles, the Artist Behind Finiliar (OpenSea blog)
_source: https://opensea.io/blog/articles/finiliar · accessed 2026-06-10_

## Key creators & collaborators
- **Ed Fornieles**: British multidisciplinary artist who created Finiliar.
- **Sam Spike**: art curator who partnered with Fornieles in January 2022.

## Project overview
Finiliar is an NFT collection of 10,000 digital pets launched in 2022. Each creature's mood fluctuates based on linked cryptocurrency performance through data oracles.

## How the mood/price mechanism works
Each Fini is tethered to a specific cryptocurrency. When a currency's value drops, the Finiliar displays sadness or illness; when it rises, the creature appears happy and energetic. Mood changes occur at varying intervals (hourly, twice daily, or weekly).

## Ten crypto families
Avalanche, Binance, Bitcoin, Chainlink, Dogecoin, Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Tezos, and Uniswap.

## Historical development
- **2016**: Fornieles created the original Fini as a GIF tethered to RSS currency feeds; exhibited in galleries globally (New York to Tokyo).
- **2022**: Launched the Finiliar NFT collection after meeting Sam Spike.

## Related collections / tools
- **Fini Sketches** (2024): 25 sketches and storyboards showing character development.
- **Oom app**: gamified community engagement app encouraging deeper conversations among holders.

## Notable quote
> "They were these cute embodiments of data that existed... What I saw in NFTs was the possibility that a piece of work might have an intimacy with thousands of people, and not just a few." — Ed Fornieles
