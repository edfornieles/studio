# CUTE — Somerset House exhibition page

_source: https://www.somersethouse.org.uk/whats-on/cute · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Exhibition:** CUTE (full framing also seen as "CUTE: An Exhibition Exploring the Irresistible Rise of Cuteness")
**Dates:** 25 Jan – 14 Apr 2024
**Location:** Embankment Galleries, South Wing, Somerset House
**Price:** £18.50 / Concessions from £11
**Status:** Archived Event
**Senior Curator:** Claire Catterall

Description: the exhibition explores cuteness in contemporary culture through artworks, music, fashion, toys, video games and social media — "from emojis to internet memes, video games to plushie toys, food to loveable robotic design." It examines "how it has become such an influential measure of our times" and seeks to "unravel cuteness' emotive charge, revealing its extraordinary and complex power and potential."

The show is organised around five themes: **Cry Baby, Play Together, Monstrous Others, Sugar-Coated Pill, Hypersonic.**

Claire Catterall (Senior Curator): "Cute is undeniably the most prominent aesthetic of our times. Yet, it has only now begun to be taken seriously" — noting its power has "slowly crept up on us."

**Featured artists:** Mark Leckey, Sean-Kierre Lyons, AYA TAKANO, Mike Kelley, Karen Kilimnik, Nayland Blake, Cosima von Bonin, Hannah Diamond, Ed Fornieles, Juliana Huxtable, Rachel Maclean, Julien Ceccaldi, Paige K. B., Isaac Lythgoe, Alake Shilling, Wong Ping, Liv Preston, CFGNY, Ram Han, Maggie Lee, Bunny Rogers, Flannery Silva, Andy Holden, plus Somerset House Studios artists Chris Zhongtian Yuan, Sin Wai Kin and Sian Fan. (Bart Seng Wen Long also exhibited; per Apollo, "specially commissioned pieces by artists including Ed Fornieles and Bart Seng Wen Long.")

**Critical reception (as listed by venue):** Evening Standard ★★★★★ "a must-visit for any adult child of the internet age"; Time Out ★★★★ "a brilliant exploration of an all-consuming cultural phenomenon"; The Guardian ★★★★ "a thoughtful exploration of the cult and culture of cuteness".

**Special features:** Games arcade; Hello Kitty 50th anniversary (Sanrio partnership); Principal Partner Dr Jart+; Hotel Partner Strand Palace Hotel.
