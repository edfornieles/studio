## On 8 Ball Sages (mechanics)

"The 8 Ball Sages use ChatGPT to create entities with whom you can have relationships that offer advice and guidance. They have moods—they can be happy, sad, or ambivalent, with their advice delivered through archetypes that might resemble traditional mystic fortune tellers, philosophers, or have a punkish attitude."

"In terms of how the moods are created, they are using hashes on the blockchain to dictate them, so trying to use the system it's contained within to dictate its vibe." (i.e. the Sage's mood is derived from its own on-chain hash data.)

Fornieles "is interested in seeing how stylisation through ChatGPT affects the advice given, exploring if a specific worldview changes the material advice."

Per the Verse exhibition listing: "8 Ball Sages uses chatbots and AI technology, creating an aggregated voice from a data set of thousands of articles and texts."

## On the lineage (Finiliar, Oom)

"In the Finiliar collection, there are 10 families, each with its aesthetic logic, with clans and small groups that echo a society—some having leaders, some being more democratic, others more human and real, while others are sci-fi and strange, or abstract and glooping."

"Fini are living NFTs that use oracles to respond to fluctuations in the value of cryptocurrencies, becoming happy and buoyant when the currency they are linked to does well, and sad or even sick when it performs poorly."

On Oom: "The artist is developing new features for Oom and will start marketing it soon, hoping it can become a widely used product by many thousands of people. Oom is an app associated with the Finis, and it's intriguing because it's unclear if Oom is a performative artwork or a revenue-generating product."
