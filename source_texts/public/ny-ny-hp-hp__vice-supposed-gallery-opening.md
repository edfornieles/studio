# New York New York Happy Happy — Vice: "I Was Bit On The Face And Ate Salami Off A Naked Person At A Supposed Gallery Opening"
_source: https://www.vice.com/en/article/vvyed4/a-supposed-gallery-opening-turned-into-a-hedonistic-nightmare · accessed 2026-06-10_

> Note: firsthand account, copyright-protected; detailed extract with short attributed quotations.

**Author:** Zach Sokol (firsthand participant). **Event:** Ed Fornieles' "NY NY HP HP," The Sky Room, The New Museum, 7 November (2013), sponsored by Rhizome.

**Ticket tiers:** ranged from $50 ("bronze") to $5,000 ("black" level), with amenities advertised including "dedicated arm candy" and limo rides.

**Persona-assignment mechanics (firsthand):** Sokol received a phone call from Fornieles the morning of the event assigning him the role "Patrick Bateman," to channel that character through the night. Other guests received roles including "Selfie," "E-Cig," and "Gossip Queen." Sokol described the concept as a "semi-fictional benefit gala" at which attendees would "adopt a heightened persona."

**Sensory / event detail:**
- Sokol arrived underdressed ("a red button-down and jeans") while others wore formal gala attire.
- Hari Nef performed a lip-synched cover; dancers released "confetti and white fluff."
- "Human meat plates were brought out" — salami served off dancers' chests.
- Performers stripped on raised platforms; Chippendales-style dancers took over the room.
- The evening climaxed in chaos: a crew member "bit me on the cheek," then wrapped arms around him and fell backward into a "human meat pile"; guests ended up "destroying the entire room," which was "completely decimated around 11PM."

**Interpretation:** the event blurred reality and performance, leaving the writer uncertain whether it was social commentary or psychological manipulation.
