Context on the relationship between *Truth Table* (2016, Basement Roma) and the
VR component shown within *Seed* (Carlos/Ishikawa, London, 22 Sep – 28 Oct 2017).

## Captured / quoted

Carlos/Ishikawa selected solo exhibitions list both:
- *Truth Table*, Cura – Basement Roma, Rome (2016)
- *Sim Vol. 1*, Alyssa Davis Gallery, New York (2017)
- *Seed*, Carlos/Ishikawa, London — 22 Sep – 28 Oct 2017

A work in the *Seed* show was described (review/search synthesis) as "a VR porn
program where the body of your lover constantly shifts gender, race and shape,"
characterised as "enforced non-discriminatory shagging, the politicisation of
onanism." Panels alongside the VR work were described as looking like "kinky
John Baldessaris."

This VR porn program in *Seed* is the same body-shifting / randomized-variable
VR premise as *Truth Table* (2016). **INFERENCE (not independently confirmed):**
*Truth Table* and the *Seed* VR component are the same or closely-related VR work
re-presented; exact equivalence and dates are fuzzy and should be confirmed with Ed.
