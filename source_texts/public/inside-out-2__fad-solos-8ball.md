# Inside Out 2 — FAD Magazine (context: SOLOS / 8 Ball Sages, references Inside Out 2)
_source: https://fadmagazine.com/2024/07/29/ed-fornieles-new-series-coming-this-september-from-solos/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Artist Ed Fornieles is releasing *8 Ball Sages*, an NFT art series curated by art historian Nico Epstein. The edition consists of 500 digital artworks, with a live minting event scheduled for September 13th at Shoreditch Arts Club in London.

The 8 Ball Sages are described as "responsive, uniquely animated characters" whose moods respond to external factors. The series draws inspiration from the classic Magic 8 Ball toy while exploring AI's role in modern interaction and questioning "its capacity to turn into a darker more menacing construct." The work references "Zoltan fun-park fortune tellers and the 2001 Sci-fi fantasy film A.I. Artificial Intelligence" to examine how technological commodification of wisdom has evolved.

Fornieles' recent work is described as including:
- **Oom**: a relationship app
- **Finiliars**: living cartoon characters whose behaviours fluctuate with currency market movements
- **Inside Out 2**: solo exhibition at Carlos/Ishikawa in London
