# The Hangover Part II — Carlos/Ishikawa exhibition page
_source: https://www.carlosishikawa.com/exhibitions/the-hangover-part-ii/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

The Hangover Part II
11 November – 17 December 2011
Carlos/Ishikawa, Unit 4, 88 Mile End Road, London E1 4UN

A publication is available for download at the gallery's website.

Works in the exhibition (titles, materials, dimensions):

- Contagion (2011) — Steel, inflatable rhino, polystyrene, 150 x 125 x 230 cm
- Monster (2011) — Hardiplank lapsiding, metal studwork, clamps, iPhone 3G, 261 x 360 x 112 cm
- Adventureland (2011) — Steel, glass, Twitter feed, computers, Spiderman costume, nylon leggings, Dorm Daze photographs, stretch Scooby-Doo, cotton candy, knife, comics, 202 x 231 x 54 cm
- The Hangover (2011) — Carpet, Animal House residue, 254 x 330 x 9 cm
- Glock (2011) — Sport socks, polystyrene, papier maché, bubblewrap, tape, 102 x 41 x 20 cm
- ORCA (2011) — Inflatable whale, steel, nylon peace flags, expanding foam, 219 x 230 x 77 cm
- Dorm Daze (2011) — Features Facebook sitcom, photographs, script book, Magic Johnson poster, sports items, and various materials
