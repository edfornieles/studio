# The Dreamy Awards — Serpentine Galleries: Park Nights: Ed Fornieles
_source: https://www.serpentinegalleries.org/whats-on/park-nights-ed-fornieles/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Date & location:** 7 September 2012, Serpentine South Gallery / Serpentine Gallery Pavilion 2012 (designed by Herzog & de Meuron with Ai Weiwei), London. Free admission. Part of the Park Nights series.

**Artist:** Ed Fornieles (b. 1983, UK).

**Event:** *The Dreamy Awards* — a participatory award ceremony examining cultural rituals and contemporary influence. It recognized figures "helping to shape our current cultural and technological landscape" across music, television, film, politics, business and technology. Actor Zac Efron received recognition alongside other honorees (Efron via a pre-recorded video clip).

**Participatory mechanic:** each attendee received an assigned character identity upon ticket purchase, becoming an active contributor. Following artist briefings or phone conversations, participants embodied their roles through the evening; character types ranged "from princess to tech legend; from political spin-doctor to superstar DJ."

**Social structure:** the ceremony established a "three-tiered micro-society" uniting public figures, the artist's collaborators, and general audience members through shared character adoption — creating "equality...through the unifying, collective act," forging social equality "through the shared experience of adopting a new character."

**Documentation:** filmed and presented as artwork on The Space (Arts Council England / BBC digital initiative). **Photographer credit:** Lewis Ronald.
