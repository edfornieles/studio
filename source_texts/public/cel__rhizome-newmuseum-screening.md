# Cel — Rhizome / New Museum screening and conversation

Event: "Cel": A screening and conversation with artist Ed Fornieles, held in association with the New Museum, New York (December 2019). Companion listing: https://www.newmuseum.org/calendar/view/1607/cel-a-screening-and-conversation-with-artist-ed-fornieles-1

Description (Rhizome): "Cel" explores the ideologies that influence aggressive expressions of masculinity through a seventy-two-hour immersive role-play performance in which ten participants navigate a fictional, embodied version of extremist online communities largely populated by white men.

Fornieles worked with LARP (live-action role-play) designer Nina Runa Essendrop to develop a set of protocols that allowed participants to improvise and freely navigate the space as their character.

The exhibition featured an immersive two-channel film presenting footage from a Live Action Role Play scenario that included the construction of a hierarchical social structure in which aggression and brutality were initially necessary for survival. The LARP session includes de-briefing interviews with participants who reflect on their experiences within the CEL.

Additional context gathered from listings/searches: participants included actors, members of the Nordic LARP club community, young male gamers, and a woman playing a male character who was a dominatrix; the group "lived in a basement apartment for three days," with the fundamental rule being "to always have someone lower than yourself." Fornieles described Cel as a sort of inversion of "second-wave feminist discourses."
