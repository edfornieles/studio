# Ed Fornieles — Full CV (assembled from public sources)
_compiled 2026-06-10 · all sources public · see Sources at bottom · items flagged (?) need review_

**Edward Fornieles.** b. 6 April 1983, Petersfield, Hampshire, UK. Lives and works in London (has also worked in Los Angeles and Montreal). Interdisciplinary artist working across film, social media, sculpture, installation and performance — immersive simulations that construct alternative social/political spaces. Represented by Carlos/Ishikawa, London.

## Education
- 2011 — MA Sculpture, Royal College of Art, London
- 2005 — BA Fine Art, Ruskin School of Drawing and Fine Art, University of Oxford
- 2001 — HND Art & Design, Winchester University (Winchester School of Art) (?)
- 2003 — Cunliffe Prize (judged by Anya Gallaccio)

## Career notes
- Studio assistant to Anish Kapoor during MA.
- Worked as a freelance data analyst, East London.
- 2007 — co-founded Wallis Gallery with Ross McNicol and Vanessa Carlos.
