# Animal House — A Three-Month College Sitcom on Facebook (Hyperallergic)
_source: https://hyperallergic.com/49463/a-three-month-college-sitcom-on-facebook/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

**Author:** AX Mina
**Published:** April 5, 2012 (updated November 24, 2025)
**Publication:** Hyperallergic

_Note: This piece principally concerns Dorm Daze, the Facebook sitcom that grew out of / paralleled the Animal House party project. Captured here as context for Animal House's escalation-and-roles method._

Artist Ed Fornieles developed *Dorm Daze*, a Facebook-based project featuring 35 fictional characters navigating three months of college life. The work examines performance and identity within social media platforms.

The project depicted campus dramatics including a college basketball star and math geek involved in a drug ring and the unrequited love of two fraternity guys. A narrator presented highlights in a manner similar to *Gossip Girl*'s storytelling approach.

Fornieles on performance and reality:
"Characters informed their navigation of that with your previous experience of the real world; of its codes, conventions and understandings" — and he noted that "you'd be neither acting nor gaming; it's more of a transferal of skills."

The work is framed as an example of how theatre explores daily human drama through digital spaces, alongside other social-media adaptations such as a Facebook *Much Ado About Nothing* re-enactment and the Royal Shakespeare Company's Twitter-based *Romeo and Juliet*.
