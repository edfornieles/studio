Overview: In 2011, Ed Fornieles started Dorm Daze, a "Facebook Sitcom" in which the characters were crafted from real-life Facebook profiles, played by actors who were left for three months to act out the lives of a group of college freshmen at Berkeley, in California.

Key quotes / concepts attributed to Fornieles:
- "Whatever is popular is powerful."
- Each performance is concerned with identity and group formation, and with what pressures are being asserted in that process. The work attempts to reveal to the participant or viewer that this is going on within themselves, creating "a pocket of space to detach and observe themselves." He thinks "this temporary point of detachment is super interesting and has contained within it a lot of potential."
- Because the characters were archetypal, each was built with a pre-existing trajectory and a generic history that could be tapped into; everyone knew their type and all the preferences and desires that hover around it. This helped participants free themselves from the anxiety of constructing their own self-image.
