# Cel — FUTURA Center for Contemporary Art, Prague (via ArtRabbit)
_source: https://www.artrabbit.com/events/ed-fornieles-c-e-l · accessed 2026-06-10_

Ed Fornieles: C E L
Dates: 10 May 2019 – 23 June 2019.
Venue: FUTURA Center for Contemporary Art, Holečkova 49, Prague 150 00, Czechia.
Curator: Christina Gigliotti.

Description (venue text): The exhibition examines "toxic and violent behaviors that arise under a patriarchal society." It features a two-channel immersive film documenting a Live Action Role Play (LARP) scenario created by Fornieles. The LARP established "a hierarchical social structure in which aggression and brutality is at first necessary for survival." The installation includes post-session interviews with participants reflecting on their experiences.

Quoted: "CEL is an exercise and attempt at understanding where destructive behavior originates from, and endeavors to offer alternatives by allowing a space for vulnerability and empathy to emerge."
