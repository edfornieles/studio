## Athens Biennale 2018 (AB6 "ANTI") — artist page

Ed Fornieles (b. 1983, London). "Creates dynamic funding models based on crypto currencies."

**The Certificate Program (2018):** "a dynamic funding model that intends to reshape the financial mechanisms sustaining an artist's studio." The work employs Ethereum blockchain technology to store and distribute value through certificates featuring embedded keys with scratch panels. Certificate holders must evaluate whether to cash out by scratching the panel, deciding between the certificate's value versus Ethereum's current value. Successive certificate generations increase in sale price at fixed intervals, enabling early supporters to benefit from lower pricing while newer investors contribute higher amounts.

- **The Certificate Program:** Cryptocertificate, 190 gr. paper, 31 × 41 cm.
- Also shown at AB6: **The Cel** (2018), video documenting an immersive LARP / real-game event by OMSK Social Club — a 72-hour experience "to engage in alternate realities that confront deep individual and collective psychological patterns."
- Photographer (installation): Nysos Vasilopoulos.

## Kate Vass Galerie — TYPE: ATHENS BIENNALE OFFICIAL CERTIFICATES (EF004)

- **Edition:** 20/30
- **Dimensions:** 31 × 41 cm
- **Year:** September 2018
- **Style/Product no.:** EF004
- **Medium:** Artist print combined with financial product (Project Certificate)
- **Status:** SOLD OUT (price listed CHF 0.00 = sold out)

"a key embedded under a scratch panel, which entitles the holder to a percentage of an ethereum fund generated through the sale of certificates and artworks." Owners face a dilemma: preserve the certificate's artistic value or damage it by scratching to access embedded cryptocurrency funds. The program uses blockchain technology to distribute and store value across a decentralized investor network.

## Kate Vass Galerie — Ed Fornieles Studios overview page

The Certificate Program is detailed across multiple product listings, all marked SOLD OUT, exhibited at **"Perfect and Priceless"** (Zürich, Kate Vass Galerie, 2018–2019):
- Studio Contract EF001 (31 × 41 cm, edition 04/30)
- Studio Contracts EF002, EF003, EF005
- Athens Biennale Official Certificates EF004 (31 × 41 cm, 20/30)

"Each certificate contains a key embedded under a scratch panel, which entitles the holder to a percentage of an ethereum fund generated through the sale of certificates and artworks." Certificates increase in size and market value across generations, rewarding early investors.

Bio note (Kate Vass): "a post-internet artist using Facebook, Twitter, Tumblr, and Instagram as raw materials," formerly a data analyst; collapses online/offline by translating web-based art into sculpture and performance. Cites Dorm Daze ("Facebook sitcom," described as "social media terrorism" for corrupting Facebook's analytics) and the Modern Family series.
