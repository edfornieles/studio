## "Reality Blurred" interview (verse.works journal) — extracted points
The interview situates Finiliar within Fornieles' broader practice (online role-play / data-scraping works such as "Dorm Daze" and "Animal House" that blur reality and fiction).

On the Finiliar at Arsenal, New York: the Finiliars are living NFTs that use oracles to respond to fluctuations in the value of cryptocurrencies, becoming happy and buoyant when the currency does well, and sad or even sick when it performs poorly.

On data and empathy: Fornieles intends to provoke a combination of **empathy and powerlessness** in the individual through the Finiliar, as the data can only be altered through the reactions of a group, whose emotions can be manipulated to effect both positive and negative ends.

> "The Finiliar is an attempt to try to give a body to infrastructure or to things that normally don't have an existence in a reality." — Ed Fornieles
