# Cel — Carlos/Ishikawa exhibition page
_source: https://www.carlosishikawa.com/exhibitions/cel/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Ed Fornieles, "Cel"
20 March – 20 April 2019 (booklet dates the run 21 March – 20 April; private view 20 March, 6–9pm).
Carlos/Ishikawa, Unit 4, 88 Mile End Road, London E1 4UN.

The page presents installation views and video stills from the work and links downloadable resources:
- Exhibition publication (PDF booklet, 2019).
- "Cel – Debrief" podcast (2019).
- Panel discussion on post-patriarchal masculinity (3 April 2019).
- Panel discussion on male identity, the alt-right, and violent extremism (17 April 2019).

The gallery page itself carries no extended press-release prose; the curatorial/critical text lives in the downloadable booklet (see cel__carlosishikawa-booklet.md). Cel was a two-channel immersive film presenting footage from a 72-hour Live Action Role Play, together with debrief interviews of participants.
