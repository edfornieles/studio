# Associations — Artvisor exhibition review
_source: https://artvisor.com/exhibition-review-ed-fornieles-association-works-at-carlos-ishikawa/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

_Review of Ed Fornieles' Associations at Carlos/Ishikawa, 17 April – 22 May 2021. Key content extracted; some passages are the reviewer's paraphrase._

From 17 April to 22 May 2021, Ed Fornieles' *Associations* exhibited at Carlos/Ishikawa, 88 Mile End Road, London.

Born in England in 1983, Fornieles is a multimedia artist employing film, social media, sculpture, installation and performance to investigate how people interact with twenty-first century popular culture.

The review notes that the project was produced by Malevich and curated by co-founder Svetlana Marich, and that the exhibition used a free-association methodology to generate image sequences where patterns emerge, shift and mutate rapidly.

Quoted from the accompanying booklet essay (sub-heading "Seeing"): "One image is followed by another until a chain is formed... I seem caught in between things."

The review states the exhibition "challenges how we as individuals look, process and absorb the images around us," employing real-time news and social media to examine contemporary power dynamics. Ben Burbridge collaborated with Fornieles on an accompanying booklet providing intellectual frameworks for interpreting the decontextualized imagery.

The reviewer describes viewers becoming "absorbed into the mesmerising chain of images, losing yourself within the maze."
