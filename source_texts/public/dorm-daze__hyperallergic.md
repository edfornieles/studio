# Dorm Daze — Hyperallergic: "A Three-Month College Sitcom on Facebook"
_source: https://hyperallergic.com/a-three-month-college-sitcom-on-facebook/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Author: An Xiao (AX) Mina. Published April 5, 2012.

Project creator: Ed Fornieles (London/LA-based artist).

Scope: 35 characters enacted a fictional three-month college experience on Facebook, featuring plotlines such as a basketball athlete and mathematics student caught in criminal activity, plus unrequited romantic tension between fraternity members.

Quote from Fornieles: "Characters informed their navigation of that with your previous experience of the real world; of its codes, conventions and understandings. So in a sense ... you'd be neither acting nor gaming; it's more of a transferal of skills."

Additional context: The project blurs distinctions between social media self-presentation and theatrical performance. A narrator in Gossip Girl style provided commentary throughout the intervention's highlights.

(Note: participant count is given as 35 here and as 34 in the Dazed/i-D accounts.)
