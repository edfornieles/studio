# Jupiter Ascending — Carl Kostyál exhibition page

_source: https://kostyal.com/exhibitions/jupiter-ascending/ · accessed 2026-06-10_

Ed Fornieles, *Jupiter Ascending*. Carl Kostyál, Stockholm (Isbrytaren). 19.02.2015 — 20.03.2015. Open Fridays and Saturdays 12–4pm (or by appointment). Contact: emmahatt@icloud.com.

The exhibition presents a functioning spa exploring themes of renewal, contemplation and the effects of living in a stream of data. The gallery frames it as "a pocket of resistance in which to reflect, review and dive back into the bombardment of global news, bio explosions, eco meltdowns and emotional matrixes." The exhibition attempts to disrupt the high-speed flow of information by creating that pocket of resistance in which to reflect and review.

**Sauna / sweat lodge.** The spa facilities are based on the Native American practice of the sweat lodge, a place of spiritual refuge used to repair damages done to both mind and body. "In the darkness of the lodge, all sense of race, colour and religion are set aside." Live news radio channels are distorted and fed into the sauna, becoming an abstracted background to personal and communal visions.

**Tea ceremony.** A selection of ancient Japanese teas grown on the volcanic island of Honshu, traditionally used in Zen Japanese tea ceremonies, is offered. These counterbalance caffeine's accelerationist effects, with L-Theanine calming the nervous system while enhancing mindfulness.

**Meditation spaces / data totems.** Communal and private meditation spaces each have their own "data totem" where breaking news stories, image feeds and graphics roll over visitors — "a reality stream of impermanent identities forming narratives."
