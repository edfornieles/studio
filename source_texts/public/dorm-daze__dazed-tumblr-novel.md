# Dorm Daze — Dazed: "Exclusive: the Tumblr novel of the Facebook college sitcom"
_source: https://www.dazeddigital.com/artsandculture/article/23483/1/exclusive-the-tumblr-novel-of-the-facebook-college-sitcom · accessed 2026-06-10_

Publication: Dazed Digital. Date: February 4, 2015.

Overview: Ed Fornieles created a performance piece on Facebook featuring 34 participants inhabiting "scalped profiles" (harvested from real UC Berkeley students' public data) over three months in late 2011. Writer Dean Kissick transformed this raw material into a Tumblr novel.

Quotes from Fornieles:
- "Dorm Daze was a performance on Facebook set in the University of California, Berkeley," involving participants who "inhabited 34 scalped profiles."
- "Everything in the Dorm Daze network started to hyper-accelerate over a period of three months," moving from sitcom realism into "fantasy, horror and romance."
- "Dorm Daze aimed to unleash latent productive forces towards the building of this new fiction."

Character archetypes: The cast included college stereotypes (sorority presidents, jocks, indie kids, goths) experiencing typical university narratives — parties, romance, conflict — entirely within fictional Facebook interactions conducted by London-based performers.

Additional research note (from related Dazed/CURA coverage): In the beginning the stories grounded themselves in the everyday of sitcom narratives; imaginary parties allowed characters to bond into groups — the frat house, the sorority, the indie kids and outsiders. Dorm Daze became "a very spongy place" that continuously absorbed new fictions and current events such as the Occupy movement and Proposition 8. One participant, Jon Ow (playing Robby Chan), used the character as a vehicle to explore romantic and sexual narratives he found hard to pursue in real life, and as a place of protest — incorporating same-sex marriage and gay rights into his personal narrative and opposing the homophobia generated by the fictional frat house. This is where the deconstruction of the archetypes began.
