# Sim Vol. 1: Existential Risk — Ed Fornieles on Art as Human Sacrifice (Le Random)
_source: https://www.lerandom.art/editorial/ed-fornieles-on-art-as-human-sacrifice · accessed 2026-06-10_

Interview/editorial (dated 2026 on page). Quotes relevant to the simulation /
role-play strand that *Sim Vol. 1* belongs to.

## Quotes (verbatim, as captured)

- "At its best it's terrifyingly emergent. Roleplay is a good example of this."
- "I often begin making work from this idea of systems building. I set up a
  bunch of parameters; then people, images or algorithms play themselves out."
- "I'm not so interested in the artist as a sovereign figure imposing meaning
  from above. I'm more interested in building a situation that can think a little
  for itself."
- "I often think art has something to do with human sacrifice: that work in part
  takes on significance because someone gives years of their life to an idea, to
  a way of thinking and, through that commitment, that bleeding, something
  meaningful might get made."

These articulate the studio's core method behind both *Sim Vol. 1* and
*Truth Table*: build a parameterized system, then let participants/algorithms
play it out toward emergent, often dark behaviour.
