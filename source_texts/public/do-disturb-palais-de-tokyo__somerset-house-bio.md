# Do Disturb / Fortress of Solitude — Somerset House artist bio
_source: https://www.somersethouse.org.uk/a-z-community-directory/ed-fornieles · accessed 2026-06-10_

This Somerset House community-directory bio is the single public source that names "The Fortress of Solitude" as a Fornieles work.

**Verbatim sentence:**

"At Chisenhale Gallery, London, he presented _Modern Family_, and at the Palais de Tokyo, Paris, he organised the performance _The Fortress of Solitude_."

**What this establishes (fact):**
- Fornieles "organised the performance _The Fortress of Solitude_" at Palais de Tokyo, Paris.

**What this does NOT establish (gap):**
- No **year** is given.
- No link to the **Do Disturb** festival is stated.
- The Palais de Tokyo and Claudine Colin Do Disturb 2016 sources name Fornieles as a participant but give **no performance title**.

**Inference (unconfirmed):** It is plausible that "The Fortress of Solitude" is the title of his Do Disturb 2016 contribution, since Do Disturb is the documented Fornieles performance at Palais de Tokyo. But no single source ties the title to the festival or to the April 2016 dates. This remains an inference requiring confirmation from Ed.

_Note: web searches for "Fortress of Solitude" are heavily polluted by the Superman/DC location and Jonathan Lethem's novel; none of that is relevant here._
