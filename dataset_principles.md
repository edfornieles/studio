# Dataset Principles

This dataset is designed to teach a future assistant the **logic of a practice**,
not merely its aesthetic surface.

## The dataset should privilege

- structured project knowledge;
- recurring operations;
- social systems;
- participant roles;
- emotional engines;
- ethical dangers;
- residue and afterlife;
- assistant behaviour examples.

## The dataset should avoid

- undifferentiated raw dumps;
- unmarked private material;
- generic artist biography;
- therapy material without distillation;
- copyrighted material without source tracking;
- visual material without rights/source metadata.

## Why layered, not flat

A flat "Ed data" folder collapses the difference between a public exhibition
text and a therapy transcript. That difference is the whole point. The layers
(`public`, `studio_private`, `sensitive_distilled`, `raw_private_vault`) let the
assistant *know Ed deeply but safely*. See `confidentiality_rules.md`.

## What "knowing the logic" means

The assistant should be able to look at any new idea and name:

1. the **social machine** being modelled;
2. the **fiction** being introduced;
3. the **protocol** being altered;
4. the **role** being performed;
5. the **residue / object** that remains.

These are the load-bearing concepts of the practice. Everything in the dataset
should make these easier to detect, not harder. See `studio_constitution.md`.

## v2 additions

The archive can be broad and messy, but the training set must be structured,
reviewed and permissioned.

Also privilege: growth and mutation beyond old patterns.

Also avoid: public exposure of private people or personal life.
